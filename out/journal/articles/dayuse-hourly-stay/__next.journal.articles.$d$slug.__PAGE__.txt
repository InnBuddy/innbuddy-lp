11:T1b27,<p>Day use and hourly stays are <strong>"selling the empty room’s hours."</strong> A room with a view of sea or mountains, a quiet lounge — even without staying the night, there is demand to "use it for three hours." This article is the foundation of day use and hourly plans: revenue from guests who do not stay.</p>

<h2>The matinee, and the evening show</h2>

<p>Kabuki and yose vaudeville performances have <strong>the two-show day</strong>. The same theater, the same seats, sold twice a day — the matinee and the evening show. The audiences change over; between the acts the stage is set; the evening show begins. Running the building called a theater twice a day — show-business wisdom continued since Edo.</p>

<p>This wisdom transfers, as is, to guest rooms. <strong>Sell the same room twice, day and night. Cleaning is the intermission.</strong> The room’s "evening show" is the stay. Then what is the "matinee"? On weekday daytime, that room belongs to no one. Day use is the design of your property’s matinee.</p>

<h2>3 demands — the matinee’s audience</h2>

<ol>
<li><strong>Remote workers</strong> — In cafés the surrounding noise bothers; at home, focus fails. Three hours of work in a room with Wi-Fi, a desk, and quiet. The guest who comes on weekday daytime.</li>
<li><strong>Couples and families between the sights</strong> — Before check-in, after check-out. Put down the bags, take a shower, breathe, and go back out. A place for the few hours in the middle of a trip.</li>
<li><strong>People from the neighborhood</strong> — Those who would rather work in nature than in a stylish building. Three hours of the extraordinary inside the ordinary, using the property’s matinee.</li>
</ol>

<h2>3 points of design</h2>

<ul>
<li><strong>Hours — two patterns, 3 and 6 hours</strong> — 10 a.m.–1 p.m., 1–6 p.m., and so on. Three hours for "work and rest"; six for "a half-day stay." Deciding and stating the time bands is the first step in conveying the use.</li>
<li><strong>Price — a guide of 50–70% of one night</strong> — For a ¥10,000 room: three hours at ¥5,000, six at ¥7,000, for instance (reference values). Cut much below 50%, and the value of "staying" starts to crumble. The matinee’s price, within the range that does not break the evening show’s world (the foundations of price are in <a href="#pricing-fundamentals">How to Set Room Rates: Pricing Fundamentals</a>).</li>
<li><strong>Included — show it with coffee, water, and Wi-Fi in</strong> — "Three hours, ¥5,000, coffee, water, and Wi-Fi included." Showing what is included first erases the anxiety about extra charges.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Designing the intermission — cleaning as a twice-daily conversion</h2>

<p>Once the matinee goes in, the property’s day changes. Cleaning is no longer a once-daily preparation but a rotation that handles <strong>two intermissions</strong> — between matinee and evening show, and after the evening show. How to set the order, how to estimate the time, the design of waiting — "the room at 3 p.m." — are handled by <a href="#room-turnover-flow">Room Turnover: Cutting Guest Wait with Cleaning Flow</a>. Only one thing must be guarded here: <strong>the cleaning after the matinee runs on the same checklist as always.</strong> Simplified cleaning will surely be found by the evening show’s first guest.</p>

<h2>The boundary of inventory — selling both shows out</h2>

<p>The hardest part of the matinee design is, in fact, inventory. A room sold for the matinee cannot be used for that night’s last-minute bookings. A day that looks full becomes, in truth, a day with fewer rooms to sell. What avoids this is <strong>the cutoff-time design</strong>. For example: "matinee bookings close at 6 p.m. the previous day." Sell the matinee without shaving the hours when evening-show bookings grow. Selling both shows out is the two-show property’s revenue management (the thinking of dynamic pricing is handled by <a href="#revpar-18x-dynamic-pricing">RevPAR Up 1.8×: Pricing From "Gut Feeling" to "Design"</a>).</p>

<h2>Telling — make the use concrete</h2>

<p>In the flyer, write not "day use available" but the uses. <strong>"For remote work, a rest, changing clothes, a shower"</strong> — with concrete uses, the reader can place themselves there. The matinee’s guest often does not realize that they are "a guest allowed to use" the property at all.</p>

<h2>The other side — when the matinee exhausts the property</h2>

<ul>
<li><strong>Cleaning caught in crossfire</strong> — Two intermissions means the cleaning stamina approaching twice over. Start with a cap on acceptance (one to two rooms a day).</li>
<li><strong>The single word "rest"</strong> — A flyer that does not state its uses remakes the property’s image on its own. Choose, in words, which guests you want to come.</li>
<li><strong>The legal and tax boundary</strong> — How non-staying use is treated — the classification of the business permit, whether the accommodation tax applies — differs by municipality and must be confirmed. Once, at the counter, before starting.</li>
<li><strong>Parking rotation</strong> — Matinee cars filling evening-show parking troubles both sets of guests. Show the time boundary to the parking lot too.</li>
<li><strong>Distance from the neighbors</strong> — The more the neighborhood uses the property, the closer it comes to being a local facility. Being welcoming is also being deliberate about distance.</li>
</ul>

<p>The first step: one room, three hours, on a weekday. Stage just that one "matinee," once, next Wednesday. When it ends, note how long the intermission (cleaning) took, and check that the evening show came to no harm. As the performances repeat, you will be able to write your property’s matinee billing in a single line. The guest room does not exist for the night alone — it is <strong>a stage performed twice a day, with an intermission between</strong>.</p>

Whether non-staying use can be offered, and its taxation, please confirm with your municipality, together with your business permit classification.</div>

12:T193c,<p>日用、计时制<strong>“出卖你的空闲时间”</strong>。对于想要在那里度过三个小时（即使不过夜）的人来说，可以看到海景或山景的房间以及看起来像一家很酷的咖啡馆的休息室很受欢迎。远程工作者希望在一个安静的地方工作，希望在观光旅行之间休息和淋浴，并希望在大自然中工作，而他们的邻居不在豪华建筑中。通过不仅满足一晚的需求，还满足“几个小时”的需求，您可以将空置房间时间转化为利润，而不浪费它。本文将组织日间使用和每小时设计，以产生无需过夜住宿的销售。</p>

<h2>1. 谁使用日间使用？三大诉求</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>远程工作者——在安静的地方工作 3 小时：</strong>我在咖啡馆里被周围的声音困扰，在家里也无法集中注意力。这样的远程工作者希望在旅馆安静的客房里工作三个小时。配备 Wi-Fi、办公桌和咖啡，是您工作 3 小时的理想场所。许多远程工作人员在工作日白天工作，这使其成为填补工作日空缺的理想选择。</li>
<li><strong>情侣/家庭 — 观光期间休息/淋浴：</strong>游览旅游目的地的情侣和家庭希望在观光旅行之间休息一下并洗澡。如果您在入住前或退房后能在房间里休息几个小时，可以缓解观光的疲劳。活动后对淋浴的需求很高，尤其是在海边或山区附近。</li>
<li><strong>邻居——在大自然中工作的地方，而不是在豪华建筑中：</strong>邻居们想要一个在大自然中工作的地方，而不是一座豪华的建筑。旅馆的休息室和客房是您可以在大自然中工作的宝贵场所。通过为附近的人们提供日常使用作为在大自然中工作的场所，您可以满足新的需求。</li>
</ul>

<h2>2. 设计三点——时间、价格、内容</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>时间 — 有 2 种模式：3 小时和 6 小时：</strong>日间使用时间有两种易于理解的模式：3 小时和 6 小时。例如：10-13:00、13-18:00、10-16:00 等。3 小时将用于“短暂休息或工作”，6 小时将用于“半天停留”。通过明确时间，客人可以更轻松地了解他们可以做什么。</li>
<li><strong>价格 — 一晚住宿的 50% 至 70%（参考值/示例）：</strong>日间使用的价格为一晚价格的 50% 至 70%。例如：每晚10,000日元的房间，3小时5,000日元，6小时7,000日元等。价格仅供参考，可能会根据地区和季节进行调整。通过明确价格，客人可以更轻松地决定是否获得优惠。</li>
<li><strong>包括 — 咖啡、水、Wi-Fi 包括：</strong>日间使用价格包括咖啡、水和 Wi-Fi。通过显示“5,000日元3小时，含咖啡、水、Wi-Fi”，可以让客人更容易理解其中的内容。通过明确包含的内容，客人可以放心“不收取额外费用”。</li>
</ol>

<h2>3. 清洁、沟通和收益管理</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>清洁 — 日常使用后定期清洁的相同清单：</strong>日间使用后的清洁工作与常规入住后的清洁清单相同。如果仅仅因为它是日间使用的房产而简化清洁工作，那么下一位客人就会不太满意。清洁清单将与常规住宿相同，但清洁时间将减少 15 分钟。</li>
<li><strong>如何沟通——具体写出目的，例如“远程办公、休息、换衣服”等：</strong>通过具体写下当天的使用目的，客人更容易了解是否可以使用。具体写下目的，例如“远程办公、休息、换衣服或洗澡”。如果目的明确，客人就会认为自己也可以使用。</li>
<li><strong>收入管理——记录日使用次数和毛利率：</strong>每月记录一次使用天数和毛利润。想象一下通过“出售空置房间时间”，白天的使用对利润的贡献有多少。日间使用收入与常规住宿收入分开管理，以可视化日间使用的价值。</li>
</ul>

<h2>行动清单</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>第一周：决定一间可供日间使用的客房。</strong> — 决定一间可供日间使用的客房。理想情况下，可以看到海景或山景的房间，或者带有安静的咖啡馆式休息室的房间是理想的选择。像往常一样使用相同的清洁清单。</li>
<li><strong>第二周：3 小时和 6 小时价格，全部包含在一张门票中</strong> — 在一张纸上总结两种模式的价格和所含内容（咖啡、水、Wi-Fi）：3 小时和 6 小时。价格定为一晚住宿的 50% 至 70%。</li>
<li><strong>第 3 周：发布在您的网站和 Google 商家资料上</strong> — 在您的公司网站和 Google 商家资料上发布日常使用信息。具体写下目的，例如“远程办公、休息、换衣服或洗澡”。</li>
<li><strong>第 4 周：创建清洁和收入管理系统</strong> — 日常使用后的清洁使用与常规清洁相同的检查表。创建了一个系统，每月记录一次使用天数和毛利润。</li>
<li><strong>延续：建立销售空置房间时间的机制</strong> — 建立日间使用作为“出售空置房间时间”的机制。即使每天只有一个房间，也会对每月毛利润产生影响。用作工作日填补空置房间的机制。</li>
