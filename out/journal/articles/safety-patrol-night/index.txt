A gas valve left unturned-off in the kitchen, a shower left running, a back door left unlocked — what someone notices by day goes unnoticed by all at night. This article is the foundation for designing the night’s "watching."</p>

<h2>Funeban — the post of "watching without sleeping"</h2>

<p>Japan’s post stations and castles had a role called <strong>funeban</strong> — the sleepless watch. As the name says: the duty of staying awake through the night, watching fire and gates. In the towns of Edo, the <strong>yomawari</strong> night watchmen walked the streets calling "Guard against fire!" Placing a person to watch the hours no one watches is, in this country, a basic design of inns and towns hundreds of years old. The night patrol is not a mechanism your property invents alone tonight — <strong>it is the modern edition of the funeban</strong>.</p>

<div class="stats"><span class="tag">FACT</span><strong>Castles and post stations kept the "funeban" — the role of staying awake at night watching over fire and gates — and in Edo’s towns the yomawari night watch patrolled the streets with the cry "Guard against fire" (hi no yōjin)</strong> — "Watching the hours no one watches" is one of the oldest jobs of the Japanese inn.</div>

<h2>What to watch — fire, water, locks, and the five senses</h2>

<ol>
<li><strong>Fire</strong> — The kitchen’s main gas valve, the hearth’s fire and ash, the smoking area’s butts. The gravest risk, and the one that grows quietest at night. Write the three points into the checklist as they are.</li>
<li><strong>Water</strong> — Leaks, winter freezing, a shower left on. Get in the habit of copying the water-meter reading, and night leaks announce themselves in numbers (one night ignored can cost a month’s water bill).</li>
<li><strong>Locks</strong> — The front door may be shut, but the back door, the kitchen door, the staff entrance, the bathroom window may be open. Check "does it not push open?" At a mountain property, against wild animals; in town, against crime — both meanings.</li>
<li><strong>The five senses (α)</strong> — Not only watching: listening, smelling. Sounds not usually there (water, machinery hum), smells not usually there (gas, scorching, mold). <strong>Smell and sound are things a camera cannot pick up</strong> — the best instrument for watching a property at night is still the human senses.</li>
</ol>

<h2>When to watch — 3 rounds and their meanings</h2>

<ul>
<li><strong>After lights-out (about 10 p.m.)</strong> — The round that guards the guests’ sleep. Check the locks and the sources of fire. No footsteps; never let flashlight light enter a guest room — <strong>this round must not become a "round that is seen"</strong>.</li>
<li><strong>Deep night (about 2 a.m.)</strong> — The hour when no one is up. Kitchen, machine room, bath faults happen most easily now. The round that guards the property itself. Do not go alone (in pairs, or leaving a record by some means).</li>
<li><strong>Toward dawn (about 5 a.m.)</strong> — The round that prepares the morning. Kitchen gas, refrigerator temperatures, drawing the baths. The last check that lands the few hours before breakfast without accident.</li>
</ul>

<p>Share with the staff that each of the three rounds has its own meaning (guarding sleep / guarding the property / making the morning), and the patrol turns from "making rounds" into a <strong>role</strong>. Small properties that cannot manage three keep the deep-night one. The hours no one watches are longest, always, in deep night.</p>

<h2>Records — the one-line patrol log</h2>

<p>The patrol log needs one line. Three fields — date, time, patroller — and three circles: fire, water, locks. A log that takes five minutes to write stops being written in three days (a record that continues is the justice). Also recommended: <strong>photograph "the normal state"</strong> in advance. The breaker positions in the panel, the usual water level of the drawn bath, the usual water-meter digits — anomaly is visible only to an eye that knows the normal.</p>

<h2>The other side — when the patrol exhausts the property</h2>

<ul>
<li><strong>The round that guards sleep breaking sleep</strong> — Footsteps, door sounds, voices, light. On the deep-night round, "being quiet" itself is the work. Choose soft indoor shoes, aim the flashlight, set the silent signals, all in advance.</li>
<li><strong>The safety of the solo night shift</strong> — If the patroller collapses on the deep-night round, no one notices. Health check-ins, a scheduled message (send one to the colleague in the other building when the round ends), how to carry the emergency contact. Before the property’s safety comes <strong>the safety of the one who watches</strong>.</li>
<li><strong>Too much surveillance</strong> — Try to replace everything with cameras, and the smells, the sounds, and "the air that differs from usual" get missed. Machines to records, humans to the five senses — divide the work.</li>
<li><strong>Becoming a shell</strong> — A log with circles marked but no rounds actually walked becomes, after an incident, the most painful piece of evidence. The log must be a device of trust, not a chain.</li>
<li><strong>Carrying it over to morning</strong> — "It can wait till morning" is the beginning of the night’s accident. Anomaly you hesitate to judge: record it and hand it to the morning — recording the hesitation itself is the manner of the funeban.</li>
</ul>

<p>The first step is writing one route tonight. Kitchen → machine room → baths → back door → front entrance. The three items — fire, water, locks — and one deep-night round to start. Make a log of nothing but dates and circles. The work of <strong>watching the hours no one watches</strong>, which inns have continued for hundreds of years — the first line of it, continued at your property from tonight.</p>

1c:T17bf,<p>安全巡逻<strong>“在没人看的时候看”而不是“醒来后看”</strong>。山上的旅馆、海边的旅馆、日本乡村的旅馆——你需要去看看它们，因为晚上没有人看它们。客人入睡后，客栈呈现出与白天不同的面貌。忘记关厨房的火，忘记关浴室的水，忘记锁后门。有人在白天注意到一切，但没有人在晚上注意到。本文概述了如何重新设计夜间安全巡逻的基础，使夜间安全巡逻不仅仅是“巡逻”，而是“安心”。</p>

<h2>1. 看什么——3“看”+α</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>火——厨房、壁炉、香烟来源：</strong>厨房里有煤气总管，忘记关掉壁炉里的火，烟蒂在吸烟区。火灾是最重大的风险。检查表指定了三项：“煤气总阀”、“壁炉的灰烬”和“吸烟区的烟灰缸”。用照片记录“正常情况”可以更容易地发现异常情况。</li>
<li><strong>水——漏水、结冰或浴室继续运转：</strong>山区旅馆的管道在冬天会结冰，海边旅馆的管道会因盐害而恶化，而日本乡村旅馆的旧管道也会漏水。如果您在夜间没有注意到，让浴室淋浴保持运转可能会浪费大量的水。养成检查水表变动的习惯。</li>
<li><strong>钥匙 — 忘记锁前门、后门或窗户：</strong>即使前门关着，后门、厨房后门、浴室窗户、员工入口也经常开锁。检查锁，确保从外面推它无法打开。忘记锁门不仅会导致盗窃，还会导致野生动物（山间旅馆的猴子和野猪，海边旅馆的猫）的入侵。</li>
<li><strong>+α 声音和气味：</strong>不仅可以看到，还可以听到和闻到。平时听不到的声音（流水声、风声）、平时听不到的气味（煤气味、烧焦味）。安全巡逻的本质是利用你的五种感官来注意到与平常不同的事物。</li>
</ol>

<h2>2. 何时观看——3个时机及其含义</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>熄灯后（晚上 10 点左右）— 客人就寝后：</strong>客人上床睡觉后，检查休息室和用餐区的火是否已点燃且门已锁好。此时的巡逻是为了“保护客人的睡眠”。请注意不要发出脚步声，并防止手电筒的光线进入客房。静静地观看。</li>
<li><strong>深夜（2点左右）——无人醒来的时间：</strong>没人看的时候。这段时间厨房、卫生间、机房更容易出现异常。 2点的巡逻是为了“保护客栈本身”。不要一个人去；始终由两个人或用相机进行记录。万一发生什么事情，它也可以作为证据。</li>
<li><strong>清晨（5点左右）——准备早餐之前：</strong>准备早餐前，检查一下厨房的煤气、冰箱的温度、浴室的热水。此时的巡逻人员正在“为早晨做准备”。在早餐工作人员到达之前，请先与他们核实一下，这样您就不会在早上的忙碌中错过任何东西。</li>
</ul>

<h2>3. 记录——用一张表看清楚，附有照片</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>如何记录：</strong>记录巡视时间和用○×看到的3点+α。不仅是○，还有照片。提前拍下正常情况的照片，以便有异常情况时可以对比。照片可以作为您所看到内容的证据。</li>
<li><strong>如果出现异常：</strong>用照片记录下来，并在第二天早上的会议上分享。即使没有异常，记录“无异常”也会让你“看到”的东西变得可见。没有记录，就不可能知道某物是否被看到。</li>
<li><strong>每月回顾：</strong>每月检查一次巡逻记录。当出现异常的时候，分析一下是什么原因造成的。如果您经常忘记锁门，请尝试更改钥匙的位置或张贴清单。巡视的目的不是“看看”，而是“带动改进”。</li>
</ul>
