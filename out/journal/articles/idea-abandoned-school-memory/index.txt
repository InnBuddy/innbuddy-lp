16:T1cff,<p>廃校という言葉には、どこか終わりの響きがあります。ですが数字で見ると、この終わりは別の始まりに変わっています。文部科学省の調査では、少子化により全国で<strong>毎年約450校</strong>の公立学校が廃校となり、2002年度から2020年度の累計は8,580校。そして現存する廃校施設のうち<strong>約8割はすでに何らかの形で活用されています</strong>（2021年5月時点、みんなの廃校プロジェクト資料）。廃校は、日本でいちばん大量に、そして着実に再生が進んでいる建物なのです。この記事は、その再生の中で宿泊施設になった先例から、廃校の記憶——机・黒板・廊下——を消さずに使う作法を考えます。</p>

<h2>1. 先例——廃校の宿は、もう半世紀の歴史があります</h2>
<p>廃校を宿にする営みは、新しい流行ではありません。静岡県西伊豆町では、廃校となった大沢里小学校を改装した町営の宿泊施設「やまびこ荘」が、1976年から営業を続けてきました。半世紀近く前から、学校を宿に転用する実験は動いていたのです。</p>
<p>もうひとつ、翻訳の参考になる例が群馬県みなかみ町の「さる小」です。2008年に廃校となった猿ヶ京小学校を改装した宿で、ピアノのある音楽室や昭和の本が並ぶ図書室がそのまま残され、給食を思わせる食事のプランや、校庭でのバーベキューが楽しめるといいます。<strong>学校の空間の文法——教室、音楽室、図書室、校庭、給食——を、宿の部屋数と施設で上書きせず、そのまま使います</strong>。これが、廃校の宿の一番の資産です。</p>
<div class="stats"><span class="tag">CASE</span><strong>廃校活用の宿の共通項は「消さない」こと</strong> — 机の落書きを削らず、黒板を黒板のまま使い、廊下を廊下のまま残す。学校の記憶をリセットして新築の宿を作るなら、わざわざ校舎を使う理由は半分以下になる。残っているからこそ、宿は「かつて学校だった場所」でいられる。</div>

<h2>2. 机と黒板——記憶を、使い続ける</h2>
<p>廃校の備品は、なにより「使われ続けること」で保存されます。</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>机</strong> — 落書きのある机をラウンジに並べます。削って塗り直した瞬間、机は「新品の古い机」になり、物語を失うのです。落書きは個人の名前が書かれていることも多く、そのまま展示するときは「卒業生の落書きをそのまま残しています」とカードで明かします——書いた本人や同級生が、大人になって宿を訪れる日があります。</li>
<li><strong>黒板</strong> — 黒板のいちばんの活用法は、教室的な使い方です。図書室のおすすめ本、星の名前、朝のメニュー——<strong>黒板に書くこと自体が、学校の記憶の続き</strong>になります。原テーマにあった「果物畑の看板を黒板にする」発想も、この文法の屋外版です。</li>
<li><strong>廊下</strong> — 学校の廊下は、幅と長さと響きが記憶を呼ぶ装置です。廊下に昔の卒業写真を並べ、ベンチを置きます。靴音の響きは、消さないほうがいいでしょう。</li>
</ul>

<h2>3. 旧校舎を宿にするときの、現実の線</h2>
<p>学校建築は「教育」に特化して作られた建物です。宿に転用するときの壁は、建物の構造と法規の側にあります。用途変更にともなう建築基準法上の手続き、旅館業法上の構造基準（客室の避難・換気・防音）、旧式の設備の更新——これらは、廃校の宿の物語の裏側にある現実です。また、過去の学校建築には、現代の基準で使えない建材（吹き付けアスベストなど）が残っている可能性があり、改修の前には専門家による調査が不可欠になるのです。体育館のような大空間は、宿としては運用しにくい面積になります。このあたりの実務は、廃校活用の実例を多く抱える自治体や、転用経験のある設計事務所の知見にあたるのが早道です。</p>

<h2>4. 廃校の机を、1つだけ買う</h2>
<p>ここまでの話は「校舎ごと」の再生ですが、原テーマにはもうひとつ、小さな入口がありました——<strong>廃校の机を1つ置く</strong>ことです。多くの自治体や施設で、廃校備品の譲渡や処分が行われています。ラウンジに廃校の机を1脚置き、「この机は、〇〇小学校で30年使われていました」というカードを添えましょう。学校の記憶は、校舎ごと借りなくても、机1脚の来歴として宿に入ることができます。このやり方なら、廃校のない地域の宿でも、近隣の学校の入れ替わりで出た机を迎えられるのです。</p>

<h2>5. 集落の記憶装置として</h2>
<p>学校が集落から消える意味は、建物のことだけではありません。学校は、子どもの声と行事で、集落の時間を刻む装置でした。朝の通学、運動会、卒業式——学校があった集落には、全員が参加する年間の予定表がありました。だから廃校の建物を残して使うことは、<strong>集落の記憶を保管する場所を残すこと</strong>でもあります。廃校の宿に集まる同窓会の多さは、偶然ではありません。卒業生にとってその建物は、自分の子ども時代が保管されている場所だからです。</p>

1b:T1c47,<p>The word "closed school" carries somewhere the ring of an ending. But in the numbers, this ending is turning into another beginning. In the Ministry of Education, Culture, Sports, Science and Technology’s survey, the falling birthrate closes <strong>about 450 public schools a year</strong> nationwide, with a cumulative total of 8,580 from fiscal 2002 to 2020. And of the surviving closed-school facilities, <strong>about 80% are already in use in some form</strong> (as of May 2021, materials of the "Minna no Hakkō — Project for Using Closed Schools"). The closed school is, in Japan, the building whose rebirth is proceeding in the greatest volume, and the most steadily. This article takes from among those rebirths the precedents that became lodging, and considers the craft of using the school’s memory — desks, blackboards, corridors — without erasing it.</p>

<h2>1. Precedent — the closed-school inn has nearly half a century of history</h2>

<p>Turning a closed school into an inn is not a new trend. In Nishiizu Town, Shizuoka Prefecture, the municipal lodging "Yamabiko-sō," converted from the closed Ōsawa-Sato Elementary School, has kept operating since 1976. The experiment of converting a school into lodging was already moving nearly half a century ago.</p>

<p>Another example useful for translation is "Saru-shō" in Minakami Town, Gunma Prefecture. It is an inn converted from the Sarugakyō Elementary School closed in 2008; the music room with its piano and the library lined with Shōwa-era books remain as they were, and guests can enjoy meals that recall school lunch and barbecues on the schoolyard. <strong>The grammar of the school’s space — classroom, music room, library, schoolyard, school lunch — is used as it stands, not overwritten with the room counts and facilities of an inn.</strong> That is the closed-school inn’s greatest asset.</p>

<div class="stats"><span class="tag">CASE</span><strong>What closed-school inns share is "not erasing"</strong> — Do not plane off the graffiti on the desks; use the blackboard as a blackboard; keep the corridor a corridor. If you reset the school’s memory and build a new inn, the reason to use the school building more than halves. It is because things remain that the inn can be "a place that was once a school."</div>

<h2>2. Desks and blackboards — memory kept in use</h2>

<p>A closed school’s furnishings are preserved, above all, by being kept in use.</p>

<ul>
<li><strong>Desks</strong> — Line the graffiti-bearing desks in the lounge. The moment you plane and repaint them, they become "new old desks" and lose their story. The graffiti often carries personal names; display them as they are, and disclose on a card: "We have kept the graduates’ graffiti as it was." The day may come when the one who wrote it, grown up, visits the inn.</li>
<li><strong>The blackboard</strong> — The best use of a blackboard is a classroom-like use. The library’s recommended books, the names of stars, the morning menu — <strong>the act of writing on the blackboard itself becomes the continuation of the school’s memory</strong>. The original-theme idea of making the orchard’s sign a blackboard is the outdoor version of this grammar.</li>
<li><strong>The corridor</strong> — A school corridor is a device whose width, length, and echo call up memory. Line it with old class photographs and place benches. The ringing of footsteps is better left un-erased.</li>
</ul>

<h2>3. The realistic lines when turning an old schoolhouse into an inn</h2>

<p>A school building is a building made for one thing: education. When converting it to lodging, the walls stand on the side of structure and regulation. The procedures under the Building Standards Act that accompany change of use; the structural standards of the Inns and Hotels Act (evacuation, ventilation, soundproofing of guest rooms); the renewal of old equipment — these are the realities behind the story of the closed-school inn. And past school buildings may retain materials unusable under today’s standards (sprayed asbestos and the like), so a survey by specialists is indispensable before renovation. A large space like a gymnasium becomes floor area that is hard to run as an inn. For this practice, the fast path is the know-how of municipalities with many closed-school conversions, or of design offices with conversion experience.</p>

<h2>4. Buying just one closed-school desk</h2>

<p>All of the above is the rebirth of a whole schoolhouse, but the original theme had one more, smaller entrance — <strong>placing a single closed-school desk</strong>. Many municipalities and facilities dispose of and transfer closed-school furnishings. Place one desk in the lounge with a card: "This desk was used for thirty years at XX Elementary School." The school’s memory can enter an inn not only by borrowing the whole building but as the provenance of a single desk. In this form, even an inn in a region with no closed schools can welcome the desks that come out of a neighboring school’s turnover.</p>

<h2>5. As the village’s memory device</h2>

<p>The meaning of a school’s disappearance from a village is not only about the building. The school was the device that marked the village’s time with children’s voices and events. The morning walk to school, the sports day, the graduation — a village that had a school had a yearly schedule in which everyone took part. So keeping the closed school’s building in use is also <strong>keeping a place where the village’s memory is stored</strong>. The number of class reunions that gather at closed-school inns is no accident. For the graduates, that building is the place where their own childhood is kept.</p>

<div class="note"><span class="tag">InnBuddy VIEW</span>What creates value in a closed school’s rebirth is not new equipment but <strong>respect for "the time that was there."</strong> Whether to erase or keep the desk graffiti, to use or to merely display the blackboard, to guard the corridor’s echo — every one of these judgments is a question not of cost but of attitude. The closed school is a teaching material that tells us: what deserves care is not the things but the time.</div>

In implementing, design under each inn’s own judgment, including safety, laws and regulations, and consideration for the neighborhood. When handling buildings, old timber, and tools, confirm structural and work safety with specialists.</div>
