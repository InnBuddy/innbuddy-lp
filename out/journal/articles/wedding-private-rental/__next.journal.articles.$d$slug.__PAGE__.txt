11:T1a65,<p>Weddings and buyouts are <strong>not "a ceremony hall" but "selling the property as a venue."</strong> What big halls cannot offer — "you can stay," "the feeling of a buyout," "nature" — properties are sought as venues for small weddings of 20–30 guests. This article is the foundation of buyout revenue from selling the property as a venue.</p>

<div class="stats"><span class="tag">FACT</span><strong>Marriages in Japan have declined continuously from the 1972 peak of about 1.10 million (1,099,984), reaching 474,741 in 2023 — roughly less than half (final figures, Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare "Vital Statistics"; 2023 was the postwar low)</strong> — In an era when the wedding market as a whole is shrinking, properties need to think about what they sell.</div>

<h2>What is being presented changes — from "formality" to "living"</h2>

<p>The hirō of a hirōen (reception) means showing widely. What the big ceremony halls presented was <strong>formality</strong>. The ceremonial stage, the rows of dishes, the order of the guests. But in an era when marriages have halved, what people ask of a venue has quietly changed. A day spent with twenty friends; the food and place the couple loves — in other words, another kind of presenting: <strong>presenting the worldview of the couple’s life together.</strong> A property buyout is chosen because it stands in the middle of this change. A property has three weapons a ceremony hall lacks.</p>

<ol>
<li><strong>You can stay</strong> — Guests from afar can lodge as they are. Travel disappears; the wedding night extends straight into the party; even the next morning’s breakfast becomes part of the wedding. Only a property can do this.</li>
<li><strong>The feeling of a buyout</strong> — No other weddings, no other lodgers. The couple’s day, borrowed with the whole world around it.</li>
<li><strong>Nature</strong> — Mountains, sea, satoyama scenery as the backdrop. Photographs last a lifetime (the power of a single photograph is handled by <a href="#hotel-photos-90">"The Photo Is 90%": What Bookable Hotel Photos Have in Common</a>).</li>
</ol>

<h2>What you sell — 3 elements</h2>

<ul>
<li><strong>All the rooms</strong> — Participants lodge as they are. Twenty guests means ten rooms. On a buyout day, the property’s occupancy fills in a single stroke.</li>
<li><strong>The common spaces</strong> — The lounge becomes reception and after-party; the dining room, the meal hall; the garden, ceremony and photographs. The property’s worldview, whole, becomes the venue.</li>
<li><strong>The meals</strong> — A course built from the land’s ingredients. Add the producers’ names, and the meal becomes a presentation of the region.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Price and terms — leave no ambiguity</h2>

<ul>
<li><strong>Show it as a package</strong> — "One night, two days, 20 guests, from ¥○○." What is included, on one sheet from the start: rooms, common spaces, meals — and whether a coordinator is provided.</li>
<li><strong>Minimum headcount and bring-your-own rules</strong> — Minimum 10 guests; bringing your own cake allowed, alcohol not — the more you write the terms in advance, the less confusion on the day.</li>
<li><strong>One couple per day</strong> — The value of a buyout is being "the only one." Set the acceptance at one couple a day, and protect the stamina of preparation.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Inviting the region into the presentation</h2>

<p>The strength of a property buyout wedding is that the region can take part. The producers of the meal, the neighborhood café for the after-party, the gardener who tends the grounds — when local people take part in the wedding day, the wedding goes beyond "the couple’s presentation" and becomes <strong>a presentation by the whole region</strong>. The basics of partnering with nearby shops are handled by <a href="#local-business-collaboration">Local Business Collaboration: Basics of Reservation-Driving Partnerships</a>. Nighttime noise and fireworks rules, and the rounds of greetings to neighbors, are the etiquette of a wedding open to the region.</p>

<h2>The other side — when buyouts exhaust the property</h2>

<ul>
<li><strong>A shrinking market</strong> — Marriages have halved. Design this not as a business chasing count, but as one that competes on the price and the memory of a single couple.</li>
<li><strong>Days that shut out regulars</strong> — The more buyout days, the harder for ordinary guests to book. Buyouts lean toward weekends — decide the division of labor with normal operations in advance.</li>
<li><strong>Staff stamina</strong> — Preparing one buyout is several times a normal meeting. Do not forget the stamina design — devoting the whole house to cleaning the day after the wedding, for instance.</li>
<li><strong>Liability and insurance</strong> — Serving alcohol, brought-in cakes, removing flower arrangements, accidents. Check the risks once, before accepting, with <a href="#hotel-insurance-risk">Hotel Insurance: Risks to Know Before They Happen</a>.</li>
<li><strong>Compatibility of worldviews</strong> — The property’s character and the wedding’s mood decide each other. Japanese-style inn, Western building, old folk house, new architecture — telling honestly "the wedding we cannot host" is part of trust too.</li>
</ul>

<p>The first step is looking at the list of past guests. Among the people who loved this property’s hours, is there no one now thinking of a wedding? People who choose a property wedding are, first, people who fell in love with the property. Deliver one flyer to that couple: "We can make your day into this property, entire." Not the business of a ceremony hall — as <strong>the business of lending a world</strong>, the buyout begins.</p>

<ul>
<li><strong>所有房间——参与者可以过夜（不可移动）：</strong>婚礼和私人聚会的参加者希望在仪式或聚会后过夜。通过出租整个旅馆，参与者可以过夜而无需搬家。来自远方的客人将能够过夜，使他们更容易参与。出租所有客房将产生收入，使客栈的入住率提高至100%。</li>
<li><strong>公共区域——休息室、餐厅和花园作为场地：</strong>公共区域休息室、餐厅和花园将用作场地。休息室可用于举办婚宴和余兴派对，用餐区可作为用餐场所，花园可用于举办仪式和拍照。通过使用公共区域作为场地，您可以推销旅馆的整个世界观。</li>
<li><strong>餐食 — 使用当地农产品和海鲜产品的课程：</strong>婚礼和私人餐点均采用当地农产品和海鲜食材制成。使用当地食材的课程为客人提供“当地风味”的体验。当食物有故事可讲时，婚礼和私人聚会会更令人满意。</li>
</ul>

<h2>2. 优势——大型婚礼堂所不具备的三点</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>您可以过夜 - 远程客人可以过夜：</strong>较大的仪式要求外地客人在仪式结束后搬到酒店。通过出租整个旅馆，远方的客人可以过夜。对于外地的客人来说，能够过夜是一个巨大的好处。</li>
<li><strong>私人感觉——没有其他客人的私人感觉：</strong>大型礼堂内还有其他婚礼和宾客。通过出租整个旅馆，您可以享受没有其他客人的私密感。对于小型婚礼和寻求私人场地的宾客来说，私密的感觉是一个很大的吸引力。</li>
<li><strong>自然——以山、海、日本乡村、海外乡村风光为背景：</strong>山峦、海洋、日本乡村和海外乡村的风景是婚礼和私人聚会的完美背景。以自然风光为背景的照片将创造终生难忘的回忆。自然是大型仪式场地无法提供的优势。</li>
</ol>

<h2>3. 价格和条件 — 显示在包装上</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>套餐内显示“2天1晚/○○人/○○日元”：</strong>婚礼和私人租赁的价格以“1晚2天，20人，50万日元起”等套餐形式显示。在套餐上显示它可以让客人更容易了解所包含的内容。套餐价格包括所有房间、公共区域和餐食。</li>
<li><strong>明确最低人数以及可以携带哪些物品：</strong>作为婚礼和私人预订的条件，我们将明确最低人数以及是否可以携带自己的物品。例如：最少10人，只允许带蛋糕进来。通过明确条件，可以防止出现问题。</li>
<li><strong>与附近的酷咖啡馆和时尚咖啡馆合作的建议（余兴派对）：</strong>对于婚礼和私人余兴派对，我们还建议与附近的咖啡馆和时尚咖啡馆合作。通过引入附近的咖啡馆作为余兴派对的场所，客人可以“在整个地区享受婚礼体验”。</li>
</ul>
