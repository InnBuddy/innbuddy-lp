11:T1a20,<p>Told to "collaborate with your neighbors," most people picture placing each other’s flyers. But a flyer left sitting brings almost no bookings. This article designs neighborhood collaboration not as "being friendly" but as <strong>"partnership that leads to bookings."</strong></p>

<h2>No single house carried it all — the toiya-ba relay</h2>

<div class="stats"><span class="tag">FACT</span><strong>Edo-period post-station towns kept a "toiya-ba" office, which handled the "tsugitate" — the relay of porters and horses on to the next post station</strong> — Travelers were not carried by one inn to the end; at each post station, the people and horses changed, and the journey was carried. Travel on the highways was a division of labor by the whole town. (Introduced as general knowledge of history.)</div>

<p>Here, one essence of collaboration is written. <strong>Collaboration was not "the sentiment of mutual aid" but "a mechanism."</strong> From the start, the Edo highways never required one house to carry everything. Around tomorrow’s property, too, stand the same partners in division of labor.</p>

<h2>The village becomes a hotel — albergo diffuso</h2>

<div class="stats"><span class="tag">CASE</span><strong>Italy’s "albergo diffuso" (scattered hotel) is a form of lodging proposed in the early 1980s by the tourism economist Giancarlo Dall’Ara</strong> — "The village square is the lobby, the streets are the corridors, the houses are the rooms." One reception; the guest rooms, the village’s empty houses; breakfast, the village bakery. Born from the revival of depopulating hamlets, it now counts about 150 member properties in Italy (per the association’s guide, as of writing).</div>

<p>Why it is interesting: the property’s "product" became not a building but the village. What the guest books is not a bed but <strong>"one more reason for one more day"</strong> — the village bakery, the farms, the square. The essence: <strong>bookings are born when there is a reason outside the property.</strong> The Japanese translation is to look, within walking distance, for the one more reason a single property cannot make alone.</p>

<h2>Whom to partner with — 3 neighbors</h2>

<ol>
<li><strong>Experiences</strong> — Mountain guides, sea SUP, satoyama harvests. The property becomes the experience operators’ "lodging department."</li>
<li><strong>Meals</strong> — The understated café, the local diner. For a small property that serves no dinner, the neighborhood’s meals are precisely the product.</li>
<li><strong>Getting around</strong> — Rental cycles, taxis, the mountain-bus. The inconvenience of moving turns into a product by collaboration.</li>
</ol>

<p>One criterion for choosing: <strong>a partner who can answer "what would the guest do if they stayed one more day?"</strong> As for partnering with the property next door and other same-industry neighbors, designing equal benefit is hard — starting with the departments your property lacks is the safe course.</p>

<h2>How to partner — 4 agreements</h2>

<ol>
<li><strong>Build one "stay + experience" package</strong> — Example: stay + wild-vegetable gathering. Not everything at first — one. The pattern of packaging is learned with this one alone.</li>
<li><strong>Place one flyer each at the other’s place</strong> — The minimal unit of mutual referral. One is enough, because more and no one looks.</li>
<li><strong>Bookings are received by the property, in one place</strong> — The guest’s booking completes in one transaction. A package that requires booking twice — once with the property, once with the experience — loses guests by that much.</li>
<li><strong>Prices: "each sells at its own price"</strong> — Stay ¥10,000 + experience ¥5,000 = ¥15,000. Splitting gross profit breeds calculation quarrels. Each party’s own price is the design that avoids the quarrel.</li>
</ol>

<h2>The one handover sheet</h2>

<p>The toiya-ba’s relay was a handover to the next post station. A property can make the same sheet. A handover note with the guest’s name and the day’s plan, passed to the partner in advance — "Mr./Ms. ○○, in your care for wild-vegetable gathering tomorrow at 8 a.m." The receiving side can more easily prepare the day’s face, and the guest feels they have been <strong>handed over</strong>. The quality of a partnership is decided less by the money arrangements than by this one sheet.</p>

<h2>The other side — 5 pitfalls of partnership</h2>

<ul>
<li><strong>Asking during the partner’s busy hours</strong> — The bakery’s first hour of morning is the floor’s busiest. Rather than asking for delivery, redesign for pickup.</li>
<li><strong>A referral lends your own credit</strong> — One sheet of introduction cannot go out to a partner whose quality you do not know.</li>
<li><strong>Blurring lines with referral fees</strong> — "Each at its own price" is the design that creates no debts of favor.</li>
<li><strong>Leaving it to the tourist board</strong> — The region’s signboard is necessary. But the property’s face is your own sheet.</li>
<li><strong>One-way referrals</strong> — At season’s end, roughly count the guests sent each way. Zero, and it is the sign to review the form.</li>
</ul>

<p>The thinking works best for properties within walking distance of a hamlet, hot-spring town, or shopping street. A lone property with no walkable partners realistically starts with mobility collaboration (sharing transfers, referring taxis).</p>

<p>The first step is walking a 500-meter radius tomorrow. If you can count three places that answer "what would the guest do with one more day?" — the partner of your first package is already decided.</p>

<li><strong>餐食——酷咖啡馆、时尚咖啡馆、当地餐馆：</strong>通过与附近的餐饮商家（如涩井咖啡厅、时尚咖啡馆、当地餐馆等）合作，您可以打造住宿+餐饮套餐。由于餐饮企业没有住宿设施，因此可以通过与客栈合作销售“住宿+餐”套餐。客栈充当餐饮经营者的“住宿部门”。</li>
<li><strong>交通——出租自行车、出租车、登山巴士：</strong>通过与附近的交通公司（自行车租赁、出租车、登山巴士等）合作，可以打造住宿+交通套餐。由于交通公司没有住宿设施，因此可以通过与客栈合作销售“住宿+交通”套餐。旅馆充当移动运营商的“住宿部门”。</li>
</ol>

<h2>2. 如何团队合作——一张协作表和相互的客户推荐</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>打造“住宿+体验”套餐（例：住宿+野菜采摘）：</strong>与附近企业合作的最简单方法是创建单一的“住宿+体验”套餐。例如：住宿+野菜采摘、过夜+SUP、过夜+收获体验等。通过打造一个套餐，我们可以了解如何与周边商家合作。</li>
<li><strong>在彼此的位置放置一张传单（相互推荐客户）：</strong>通过与邻近企业合作，您可以通过在彼此的位置放置一张传单来相互发送客户。通过将体验运营商的传单放置在旅馆中，并将旅馆的传单放置在体验运营商的位置上，您可以互相推荐顾客。一张传单就足够了。</li>
<li><strong>预订“客栈一次性接收”（客人一次即可完成预订）：</strong>与邻近企业合作时，旅馆一次性接受所有预订非常重要。客人无需单独向客栈和体验商进行预订；他们可以通过在旅馆进行一次预订来预订“住宿+体验”套餐。接受批量预订的旅馆为客人提供更多便利。</li>
</ul>

<h2>3. 毛利如何分配——每个人按自己的价格出售</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>当“每个人按自己的价格出售”而不是“分毛利”时，问题会更少：</strong>与邻近企业合作时，经常会出现毛利如何分配的问题。如果“分毛利”，毛利的计算方法就会遇到麻烦。让每个人按自己的价格出售可以避免麻烦。以住宿的价格出售住宿，以体验的价格出售体验，我们可以尊重彼此的价格。</li>
<li><strong>住宿按住宿价格，体验按体验价格：</strong>我们与周边企业合作，以旅馆的价格出售旅馆，以体验的价格出售体验。例如：如果住宿费用为 10,000 日元，体验费用为 5,000 日元，则套餐为 15,000 日元。住宿将获得10,000日元，体验经营者将获得5,000日元。通过按相互价格出售，麻烦就会减少。</li>
<li><strong>邻里协作的本质是“打造一个区域的旅程”：</strong>邻里协作的本质是“打造区域单一旅程”。通过将住宿、体验、餐饮和交通结合起来，客人可以体验“该地区独一无二的旅行”。一个套餐将增加整个地区的预订量。邻里协作不是“相处”，而是“合作有保留”。</li>
</ul>

<li><strong>第 1 周：列出 3 个当地体验、餐饮和交通</strong> — 列出附近的三个体验商家（登山向导、海洋SUP、里山丰收体验等）、食品商家（寿司咖啡馆、时尚咖啡馆、当地餐馆等）和交通商家（自行车租赁、出租车、登山巴士等）。</li>
