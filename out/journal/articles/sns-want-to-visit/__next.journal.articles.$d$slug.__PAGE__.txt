11:T16bc,<p>The aim of social media is not "awareness" but <strong>"desire"</strong>. The post that ends at "how lovely," and the post that creates "I want to go" — it is the latter that makes bookings. This article organizes the design of a post that turns awareness into desire. The design of the account as a whole (the 9-grid, the pillars, saves as the metric) belongs to the Instagram-design article. The charge here is <strong>the conditions under which a single post creates "I want to go."</strong></p>

<h2>The 2 conditions of desire</h2>

<ul>
<li><strong>Being able to imagine your own life there</strong> — Not a photograph to admire, but one in which "I spend time here" can be imagined.</li>
<li><strong>A reason to go now</strong> — Season, limited editions, blossoms, harvests — a reason to head there at this very moment.</li>
</ul>

<p>Only a post with both creates "I want to go." With only one, it stops at "how lovely." A photograph that is merely beautiful and carries no time, a photograph that has the information but no imaginable life — neither gets beyond awareness.</p>

<h2>The 3 materials that create "imagination"</h2>

<ol>
<li><strong>Traces of a person</strong> — An over-tidied empty room does not look like "my room." Steam rising, a book mid-read, a cushion slightly disturbed. The same principle as the "lived-in warmth" in the photos article — the guest’s brain simulates "I spend time here."</li>
<li><strong>Within reach</strong> — A distant view is a vista; a close-up photograph is a sense of touch. One shot taken at the height of the tabletop, the edge of the frame, an arm’s reach, becomes a photograph of "being there" more than a photograph to look at.</li>
<li><strong>The flow of time</strong> — Morning light, a window through which the sound of rain seems audible, the preparations of late afternoon. Make a still image feel time, and "time spent there" becomes imaginable.</li>
</ol>

<p>A post is the property’s <strong>peephole</strong>. When, through the peephole, "my next day off" is visible, a person presses save. A save is entering the shortlist — the design beyond that point is handled in the saveable-account article.</p>

<h2>The 4 formats that create desire</h2>

<ol>
<li><strong>The moment of first impression</strong> — The instant the door opens; the scene of the first sit. The entrance to imagination is the same as the entrance to the experience.</li>
<li><strong>The backstage</strong> — The staff’s prep, the producers’ preparations. Invisible labor moves people (it is also material that connects to hiring PR).</li>
<li><strong>Seasonal limited</strong> — "This season only." The standard-bearer of reasons to go now. But the discipline of the limited is as in the seasonal-dish article — it works only with the promise of preparing, every season, the next "only now."</li>
<li><strong>People’s reactions</strong> — A staff member’s one line, a moment of laughter. Warmth shows in photographs.</li>
</ol>

<h2>Rhythm and KPIs</h2>

<ul>
<li><strong>Seasonal campaigns, once a month</strong> — State "the reason to book now" explicitly, with the booking link.</li>
<li><strong>Weekly Stories</strong> — Run the backstage. Build the sense of "a property that is alive."</li>
<li><strong>KPIs are saves, shares, "I want to go" comments, and the booking rate via social</strong> — Follower counts are not a sufficient proxy for these four.</li>
</ul>

<h2>The other side — when designing desire exhausts the property</h2>

<ul>
<li><strong>Mass-producing "lovely"</strong> — An account that only adds beautiful photographs ends as a property that is looked at. Check every post: "Is my day off in this picture?"</li>
<li><strong>The burden of appearing</strong> — Consent for material showing staff or guests is confirmed at every shoot. Review monthly whether continuing to post has become a burden on them (a discipline shared with the hiring-PR article).</li>
<li><strong>Faking "only now"</strong> — A post that is "only now" at all times kills the word "limited." Keep the simultaneously running reasons-to-go-now to one.</li>
<li><strong>The aging of the landing place</strong> — When what desire created leads to an old page, the hard-won "I want to go" goes cold. Update the posts and the landing page (the direct site) at the same speed.</li>
<li><strong>You need not do all 4 formats</strong> — Two that fit the property’s axis are enough. Two, done richly, over four, done thinly. Formats are tools, not molds the property must obey.</li>
</ul>

<p>The first step is to look back over ten of your current posts, yourself. The question for each: "Is my day off inside this photograph?" If even one shows "a self spending time," that is your property’s mold. Running "I want to go" means increasing those molds — showing, with each post, your next day off beyond the peephole.</p>

12:T1025,<p>SNS的目的不是“认可”而是“渴望”。本文讲解了让人想去的发帖格式和节奏，将认可转化为欲望的设计。</p>
<h2>一、“认知”与“欲望”的区别</h2>
<p>“这很好”（认可）和“我想去”（欲望）——后者会产生保留。欲望通过两种方式发挥作用：</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>“我可以想象自己的生活。”</strong> — 这不仅仅是欣赏事物，而是能够想象自己在那里度过时光。</li>
<li><strong>「现在去的理由」</strong> — 季节、限量版、活动——这一刻的原因。</li>
</ul>
<p>只有具备这两个要素的帖子才会让人想去那里。</p>
<h2>2. 创造欲望的4种形式</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>“第一印象的那一刻”</strong> ——打开门坐下那一刻的场景。</li>
<li><strong>“幕后花絮”</strong> — 员工/流程/生产者。无形的努力感动人。</li>
<li><strong>「季节性限定」</strong> —“仅限这个季节”—为什么现在就去？</li>
<li><strong>“人们的反应”</strong> ——工作人员的一句话，客人的一个眼神。温暖体现在照片中。</li>
</ol>
<h2>3、发帖节奏</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>5柱固定比例</strong> — 空间、客房、食物、环境和人（与 Instagram 设计相关）。</li>
<li><strong>每周故事</strong> ——背面。营造一种“活着”的感觉。</li>
<li><strong>每月“季节性活动”</strong> — 明确“现在预订的原因”（附有预订链接）。</li>
</ul>
<h2>4.关键绩效指标</h2>
<ul>
<li>而不是关注者数量：<strong>保存数/分享数/“想去”评论/SNS预约率</strong>。</li>
<li>例：保存次数+30%，SNS预订15%（参考值）。</li>
</ul>

<h2>为什么现在值得关注？</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>客人期待的变化：</strong>当谈到“创造想要在SNS上访问的人”时，客人的期望与五年前相比发生了变化。过去“有了更好”的服务，如今已变成“没有就难以被选择”。适应这些变化，是住宿设施持续经营的前提。</li>
<li><strong>与地区关系：</strong>运营SNS的努力将增加整个地区的价值，而不仅仅是旅馆本身的价值。通过与当地生产者和周边商户合作，旅馆的行动会成为“当地故事”。社区共同参与，才更容易长期持续。</li>
<li><strong>不增加员工负担：</strong>新举措不应增加员工负担。通过建立机制，用一张表或每次15分钟的学习来运转，就能在不增加员工负担的情况下持续改进。</li>
</ul>

<h2>避免失败的3个要点</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>从小处开始——先做好一件事：</strong>一开始就想全部改变，往往难以持续。先从一件事开始。可以从一间客房、一个货架或一句话开始；即使效果不理想，影响也小，更容易调整。</li>
<li><strong>记录——用一张表看清楚：</strong>把行动记录在一张表里，效果就能被看见。你会知道哪些做法有效、哪些需要调整，记录也会成为下一轮改进的依据。</li>
<li><strong>持续——每周一次，每次15分钟：</strong>新行动需要持续。每周留出15分钟，逐渐形成习惯，最后才会成为团队文化。</li>
</ol>
