11:T175a,<p>You post a property’s video on TikTok and the plays do not grow. The reason, most often, is that you are selling "a beautiful property." What TikTok asks for is not "beauty" but <strong>the impulse of "I want to go."</strong> This article: three principles of a video that completes in 15 seconds, and the things a property should not do.</p>

<h2>Young travelers stopped searching on maps</h2>

<div class="stats"><span class="tag">RESEARCH</span><strong>In July 2022, a Google executive said at a conference that "almost 40% of young people, when looking for a place for lunch, turn to TikTok or Instagram rather than Google Maps or Search"</strong> — A statement based on the company’s internal research, widely reported as a figure showing the young generation’s "search" moving from maps to video (remarks at Fortune Brainstorm Tech, per media reports).</div>

<p>What this number means: <strong>a property’s TikTok video is not an advertisement but "a search result."</strong> When a young traveler searches "stay in ○○," your property’s 15 seconds appear there. More than whether the video is beautiful — <strong>whether it stops the eye of the person searching</strong>: that is the design we take up.</p>

<h2>The country that built the world’s shortest poetry in 17 syllables</h2>

<p>Japan holds the world’s shortest completed literature. Haiku — 17 on (syllables). A kigo places the season; a kireji ("ya," "kana") places the feeling. In just 17 syllables, this country has kept writing, for over 300 years, poems that make you "want to be there, though you have never been." What a 15-second video shares with haiku is not length but <strong>design</strong>. Not telling everything — placing only one season and one feeling. The manners of that shortness, haiku perfected first.</p>

<h2>What TikTok asks for — 3 impulses</h2>

<ol>
<li><strong>The impulse of "sound"</strong> — Waves, an egg cracking, beer being poured, firewood crackling. TikTok is a platform of sound. With sound, the viewer is "there." A beautiful image without sound is weak on TikTok.</li>
<li><strong>The impulse of "want to go"</strong> — Not "what a beautiful property" but comments that say "I want to go!" For that, you need not the perfect property but <strong>a small opening</strong>. Staff laughing, a dog running, a curtain stirring in the wind — it is because it is not perfect that one wants to go.</li>
<li><strong>The impulse of "I want to try that"</strong> — "I want to copy this breakfast." Videos that invite copying get saved, and saves connect to the next discovery.</li>
</ol>

<h2>3 principles of the 15-second video</h2>

<ol>
<li><strong>Seconds 0–3: make "what is this?"</strong> — Fail to catch interest in the first three seconds, and the video is swiped away and done. The moment the egg cracks, the moment of the dive into the pool, the moment the firewood catches — like a haiku’s kireji, place one feeling first.</li>
<li><strong>Seconds 3–12: make "I want to go"</strong> — The making of breakfast, the view from the room, the pour at the brewery. Cut with tempo, and use the sounds of the scene as they are instead of leaning on BGM. Like the 5-7-5 rhythm, tempo works as form.</li>
<li><strong>Seconds 12–15: make "the next action"</strong> — "This breakfast is tomorrow’s breakfast." "Details in the profile." Place one exit in the last three seconds, and the video conveys that beyond it lies a trip.</li>
</ol>

<h2>What a property should not do</h2>

<ul>
<li><strong>Do not post drone footage</strong> — Beautiful drone shots work on Instagram, but on TikTok they look like "ads" and get swiped away. The rule is footage with "the warmth of a human hand," shot handheld on a phone.</li>
<li><strong>Do not chase perfect staging</strong> — Footage with no gaps removes the reason to go. Do not try to shoot well.</li>
<li><strong>Do not film others without permission</strong> — Staff of course; where other guests or local people appear, always obtain consent. A property’s trust cannot be sold for one video.</li>
<li><strong>Do not handle music rights loosely</strong> — What can be used with in-app functions differs from what a business account may use. Confirm the operating rules first.</li>
<li><strong>Do not aim to go viral</strong> — Two videos a week for three months; one in eight landing is fine. Stacking saved videos is storing "search results" (the mechanics of discovery: <a href="#video-marketing-3-videos">Video Marketing: The Three Videos a Property Should Make First</a>; the design of saves: <a href="#reels-saveable-travel">Saveable Reels: How to Be Chosen Before the Trip</a>).</li>
</ul>

<p>The first step is filming one "sound" of tomorrow’s breakfast. The crack of the egg, the pour of the coffee, the crackle of the firewood. Fifteen seconds, handheld, the sound placed in the first three seconds like a kireji. A property in the country that built the 17-syllable poem has, by birth, the makings of a 15-second poem.</p>

12:T1549,<p>即使我把我的客栈的视频上传到TikTok上，浏览量也没有增加。原因是<strong>因为我们卖的是“美丽的住宿”</strong>。人们在TikTok上寻找的不是“美丽”，而是“想去的冲动”。只需 15 秒，它就会让你思考，“哇，这是什么，我想去那里？”与其说是一段美丽的无人机视频，不如说是早餐时打破鸡蛋的那一刻，跳入无边泳池的声音，或者柴火的劈啪声。本文概述了15秒内可以完成的视频的三个原则以及酒店不应该做的事情。</p>

<h2>1. TikTok想要什么——3个冲动</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>“声”的冲动：</strong>TikTok是一个健全的平台。海浪声、鸡蛋破裂声、倒啤酒声、柴火燃烧声。当有声音时，观看者会感觉自己“身临其境”。没有声音的精美视频在 TikTok 上是无力的。</li>
<li><strong>催促“走”：</strong>我希望人们说“我想去那里！”而不是“那是一家漂亮的旅馆。”为了实现这一目标，我们需要的是“轻微的差距”而不是“完美的住宿”。工作人员欢笑，狗奔跑，窗帘在风中摇曳。它并不完美，所以我想尝试一下。</li>
<li><strong>呼吁“模仿”：</strong>“我想复制这份早餐。”“我想复制这种进入泳池的方式。”您想要模仿的视频将被保存。一旦存储，它就会使用算法进行传播。只是美丽的视频将不会被保存。</li>
</ul>

<h2>2. 15秒视频的3大原则</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>做出“这是什么？” 0-3 秒内：</strong>如果你不让人们思考“这是什么？”前3秒，他们会刷你。例子：当你打碎一个鸡蛋时，有两个蛋黄；当你跳进游泳池时，彩虹出现；当你点燃木火炉时，你会听到噼啪声。在前 3 秒添加“惊喜”。</li>
<li><strong>在 3-12 秒内创建“我想去”：</strong>在 3 秒内吸引人们后，让他们在最多 12 秒内说“我想去那里”。如何做早餐、从房间看到的风景、在啤酒厂倒啤酒。快进和剪切的节奏很好。不使用背景音乐，仅使用场景中的声音。</li>
<li><strong>在 12-15 秒内创建“下一步行动”：</strong>在最后3秒内催促下一步行动。 “这份早餐是明天的早餐。”“这个泳池可以保留供私人使用。”“点击个人资料中的链接了解详细信息。”当下一步行动明确时，人们更有可能切换到您的个人资料。</li>
</ol>

<h2>3. 旅馆不应该做什么</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>请勿上传无人机镜头：</strong>美丽的无人机镜头在 Instagram 上效果很好，但在 TikTok 上，它看起来像广告，会被刷屏。对于TikTok来说，重要的是感觉它是被一个人拿走的。使用智能手机手持拍照。如果有点模糊也没关系。</li>
<li><strong>无描述：</strong>请勿包含诸如“这家酒店是......”之类的解释。我将把解释留给个人资料和链接。视频只需“冲动”即可。如果加上描述，它就变成了“广告”。</li>
<li><strong>不要每天都提：</strong>如果每天增加，质量就会下降。一周两次就好了。仔细制作一件。每周 2 个视频 x 15 秒 = 30 秒，每月 8 个视频。如果八个中的一个点击的话，浏览量就会超过一万。</li>
</ul>

<div class="stats"><span class="tag">计算示例</span><strong>例如，每周2条×3个月＝26条视频，粉丝从0增长到1200人，相当于每周约100人的速度。预订能否每月增加2～3单，很大程度取决于主页链接以及“让人想预订”的视频占比（均为示意示例）。</strong> — TikTok不是一个病毒式传播的地方，而是一个你可以收集你想访问的东西的地方。持续有效。判断效果时，请用相同的统计方法记录并比较施策前后30天的粉丝数与通过主页链接的预订。</div>

<div class="note"><span class="tag">InnBuddy 笔记</span>抖音的价值是什么？<strong>“年轻人的旅行”</strong>它位于 20 多岁的人们在 TikTok 而不是 Google 上搜索“他们想去的住宿”时。不要看漂亮的图片，而要产生去一个有声音和冲动的地方的愿望。首先，工作人员的智能手机就足够了。</div>

<li><strong>第一周：收集声音</strong> — 收集旅馆内的 3 个“声音”。海浪声、鸡蛋破裂声、倒啤酒声、柴火燃烧声。用您的智能手机录制声音，看看哪些声音让您想去那里。</li>

<li><strong>第 3 周：发布和验证</strong> — 每周 2 个帖子。分析带有“我想去”评论的视频。记录哪些声音和时刻让您想去。</li>
<li><strong>第 4 周：轮廓引导线</strong> — 在您的个人资料中，写下“这份早餐是明天的早餐。点击链接了解更多信息。”该链接是我们公司网站的试用页面。通过配置文件衡量访问权限。</li>
