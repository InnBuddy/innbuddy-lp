11:T1f9f,<p>Most properties that begin introducing themselves to media and YouTubers as "a story of getting free publicity" run aground on a case of mistaken purpose. The inn that wants publicity asks "please show us in a good light"; the asked side produces an advertisement; the audience sees through its thinness. This article sets a different definition from the starting point: an introduction to media and YouTubers is <strong>not "free advertising" but "a content partnership"</strong> — and since October 2023, this definition is no longer a matter of taste; it has become a legal line as well.</p>

<h2>October 1, 2023 — the day the line was drawn</h2>

<p>A property provides a stay free of charge, and in return a video or article introducing it is made. This form has, since October 1, 2023, had a new line drawn under the Act against Unjustifiable Premiums and Misleading Representations. An introduction made in return for a complimentary stay provided for publicity is treated not as "an independent third party's opinion" but as <strong>a representation by the business (the property)</strong>, and disclosure that it is advertising is required. An introduction without disclosure becomes a hidden advertisement — stealth marketing.</p>

<div class="stats"><span class="tag">FACT</span><strong>Stealth marketing was designated a misleading representation under the Act against Unjustifiable Premiums and Misleading Representations by a Consumer Affairs Agency notice effective October 1, 2023. When a property provides complimentary lodging for publicity and the recipient posts an introduction serving that purpose, the post is treated not as "an independent third party's opinion" but as a representation by the business (the property), and disclosure of the advertising relationship — "complimentary stay," "PR," and the like — becomes required. The party held responsible for violations is the requesting business, subject to cease-and-desist orders</strong> — The structure of "getting free publicity" can, in law, no longer be hidden.</div>

<div class="stats"><span class="tag">DID YOU KNOW?</span><strong>Posts made before the effective date also fall under the regulation if their display continues today</strong> — If an introduction made in return for a complimentary stay is still public, its labeling can still be reviewed now.</div>

<p>This line, in fact, does not lower the value of a partnership. Disclosed introductions still work — audiences who see "provided by" still watch to the end if the content is honest. The assumption that disclosure kills the effect is usually just the audience's eye for spotting staged content, projected onto your own inn. <strong>Can you make content that stands even when written honestly?</strong> That is the true condition of a partnership.</p>

<h2>The difference from filming requests — from them, versus from you</h2>

<ul>
<li><strong>Location shoots come from them; introductions come from you</strong> — Television and magazine filming requests are passive work that arrives by the production side's circumstances (handling them is another article). A property introduction is aggressive work: you choose the counterpart and reach out. Both involve "being filmed," but the ground you stand on differs.</li>
<li><strong>The goal is not "coverage" but "a continuing relationship"</strong> — An introduction that ends with one video is no different from running an advertisement once. The fruit of a partnership lies in the relationship where the other side remembers your inn as "a place worth filming again." The first reception creates the second visit.</li>
</ul>

<h2>The three steps of approach</h2>

<ol>
<li><strong>Research — find counterparts whose audience overlaps your guests</strong> — The criterion is not subscriber count but who is watching them. A city business hotel does not fit a maker of mountain-walking videos. Watch past work and check whether the audience and your guest layer overlap.</li>
<li><strong>The pitch — not "please come" but "a concept"</strong> — "Please come stay with us" is a request that is hard to answer. "Coffee brewed with mountain water, and chairs set before a bonfire — how about filming 'a cup in nature'?" becomes a seed for their planning. Write the pitch as the multiplication of the inn's appeal × the content's axis.</li>
<li><strong>Post-shoot agreements — rights, timing, reuse, disclosure</strong> — Usage rights to the photos and video (can you use them on your site and social?), the timing of the post, and the disclosure of the complimentary stay. Disclosure, beyond being a legal requirement, is the signboard that proves this partnership's trust. Set the terms before the shoot, in one email on record.</li>
</ol>

<h2>Three points of measurement — view counts not included</h2>

<ul>
<li><strong>Booking inquiries after the post</strong> — View counts are the counterpart's asset; inquiries are the inn's. For two weeks after the post, record the rise and fall of inquiries.</li>
<li><strong>Searches for the inn's name and the region</strong> — With Google Trends and the like, compare the thirty days before and after the post. The plainest evidence of whether recognition is spreading.</li>
<li><strong>Growth in direct bookings</strong> — When the call comes saying "I saw that video," or a booking arrives through your own site, that is the shape of the partnership's result. Count them separately from OTA bookings.</li>
</ul>

<p>When measuring, record the thirty days before and after the measure with the same counting method. Deciding how to compare, before the numbers, is the minimum preparation for reading the effect later.</p>

<h2>The other side — when partnerships break</h2>

<ul>
<li><strong>Drunkenness on view counts</strong> — The moment play counts rise, swapping the purpose for the number. If inquiries and direct bookings do not move, the introduction is not working as anything but an advertisement.</li>
<li><strong>The absence of courage to decline</strong> — Accepting a proposal from a counterpart whose audience does not overlap, out of obligation or momentum. A mismatched introduction only wastes both sides' time.</li>
<li><strong>The burden on the front line</strong> — Filming takes place on top of other guests' quiet and the staff's working hours. Set the hours, the filming range, and the handling of other guests in the frame — in advance, and keep them.</li>
<li><strong>Shooting on a verbal promise</strong> — Settling the shoot before fixing rights, reuse, and disclosure means redoing all of it afterward. One email is enough — put it in writing.</li>
</ul>

<p>"Would a small inn like ours even be taken seriously?" — this worry is usually just a matter of reaching out to the wrong counterpart. The first counterpart can perfectly well be a video creator with ten thousand subscribers, walking and filming the town your inn is in. Start by listing three counterparts whose audiences overlap your guests, and writing one line of concept for each. Only partnerships that do not fear disclosure will remain, from here on.</p>

12:T1a04,<p>向媒体和 YouTube 用户介绍住宿<strong>“内容合作”而不是“免费广告”</strong>。许多民宿将介绍媒体和YouTuber视为“接受免费广告”，但从不同的角度来看，这是“内容合作”。媒体和YouTuber创造“内容”。旅馆成为“内容材料”。通过将旅馆作为“内容”提供吸引力，媒体和 YouTube 用户可以创建“观众会喜欢的内容”。本文整理了将住宿介绍设计为媒体和 YouTube 用户作为内容合作伙伴关系而不是免费广告的方法。 （以下数字为参考值和示例。）</p>

<h2>1. 与“位置请求”的区别——从这里到那里</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>如需位置请求，请单击此处；如需住宿信息，请单击此处：</strong>外景请求是电视和杂志制作公司要求我们进行外景拍摄的请求（请参阅外景文章）。为了介绍一家旅馆，我们请媒体和YouTuber来介绍我们。位置请求是“被动的”，而住宿介绍是“主动的”。</li>
<li><strong>目的从“发表”变为“持续关系”：</strong>位置请求的目的是“发布”，而旅馆介绍的目的是创建“持续的关系”。不仅仅是一次性的介绍，持续的介绍会增加你的旅馆的知名度。建立持久的关系是引入住宿的本质。</li>
<li><strong>“内容合作”而不是“免费广告”：</strong>如果您考虑将您的住宿作为“免费广告”向媒体和 YouTube 用户介绍，那么您将收到“免费广告”，而媒体和 YouTube 用户会将其视为“广告”。如果你将其视为内容合作伙伴关系，则意味着“共同创建内容”，媒体和 YouTuber 将其视为“内容”。被视为内容可以提高受众的可信度。</li>
</ul>

<h2>2. 三步走——研究、提案、后期拍摄</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>研究——寻找适合寄宿家庭的创作者和媒体：</strong>我们寻找与民宿细分市场相匹配的创作者和媒体（例如山、海、自然、可持续、农业、酷咖啡馆、日本乡村）。即使你被介绍给不适合你的细分市场的创作者或媒体，观众也会觉得他们不感兴趣。寻找适合您细分市场的创作者和媒体是引入住宿的第一步。</li>
<li><strong>提案——不是“我想让人们来”而是“概念”（诉求轴+这家旅馆的内容）：</strong>当向媒体和YouTuber提出建议时，我们是基于概念，而不是希望他们来。通过提出“这间客栈的魅力（比如用山水冲泡的咖啡）+内容轴（比如设计一个在大自然中享受的杯子）”的概念，媒体和YouTuber会觉得“我们可以用这个概念来创造内容。”如果你有一个概念，媒体和YouTuber就会把它当作自己的概念。</li>
<li><strong>拍摄后——提前了解权利、发布时间以及公司内部的使用情况：</strong>为了避免拍摄后出现问题，请提前决定权利（照片/视频的使用权）、发布时间（何时发布）和内部使用（可以在您公司的网站或SNS上使用）。通过提前决定，可以避免拍摄后出现问题。权利和发布时间将在合同中指定。</li>
</ol>

<h2>3. 衡量效果的三点——询问量、关键词搜索量和直接联系量的增加</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>发布后的预订查询（根据浏览量）：</strong>向媒体和 YouTube 用户介绍住宿的有效性不是通过观看次数来衡量的，而是通过发布后的预订查询来衡量的。即使你的浏览量很大，如果没有预订查询，你的客栈会变得更出名，但不会带来预订。在发布后两周内跟踪预订查询以衡量有效性。</li>
<li><strong>关键词搜索量增加/减少：</strong>发帖后关键词搜索量的增减也是衡量效果的一个指标。如果旅馆名称或地区名称的搜索次数增加，则旅馆的认知度增加。使用 Google Search Console 或 Google Trends 检查关键字搜索的增加或减少。</li>
<li><strong>增加直接连接：</strong>发布后直接联系（通过您自己的网站预订）的增加也是衡量有效性的指标。您拥有的直接联系越多，您无需支付 OTA 费用就能获得的预订就越多。直接连接的增加将对旅馆的收入产生直接影响。</li>
</ul>

<h2>行动清单</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>第 1 周：列出 5 名适合该细分市场的创作者/媒体</strong> — 适合自托管细分市场的 5 个创作者和媒体列表（山脉、海洋、自然、可持续发展、农业、酷咖啡馆、日本乡村）。检查过去的帖子和文章，看看该细分是否适合。</li>
<li><strong>第 2 周：撰写带有概念的提案</strong> ——以“这家客栈的吸引力+内容为轴”的理念，为媒体和YouTuber写一份提案。我不会说“我希望你来”，而是以“你想一起创建内容吗？”的形式提出建议。</li>
<li><strong>第 3 周：首次介绍（1-2 人）</strong> — 询问一两个适合您细分市场的创作者或媒体机构进行初步介绍。拍摄前，请事先决定权利、发布时间以及公司内部的使用情况。</li>
<li><strong>第 4 周：创建衡量有效性的系统</strong> — 创建了一个系统来衡量预订查询、关键字搜索以及发布后直接联系的增加/减少。发布后两周跟踪效果。</li>
<li><strong>连续性：建立持久的关系</strong> — 通过不断收到推荐而不是仅仅一次访问来提高对旅馆的认知度并增加预订量。建立持久的关系是引入住宿的本质。</li>
