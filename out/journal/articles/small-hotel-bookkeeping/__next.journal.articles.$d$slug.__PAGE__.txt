11:T1ab9,<p>Hear "bookkeeping," and many small properties picture something to hand the accountant at tax time. But bookkeeping’s true work is more everyday: <strong>the monthly health check</strong>. Numbers are read not to praise effort but to notice changes in the property’s condition early. This article is about reading the three numbers the owner should see monthly — revenue, gross profit, cash — for yourself, not for the tax office (what follows is a general outline).</p>

<h2>Seeing one transaction in two faces</h2>

<div class="stats"><span class="tag">CASE</span><strong>In 1494 in Venice, a book by the friar Luca Pacioli is said to have first described double-entry bookkeeping systematically</strong> — Every transaction is recorded in "two faces": if cash decreases, something increases. A single ledger can lie, but the paired ledgers, unless they mesh, break down (as a chapter within the mathematical work "Summa").</div>

<p>The reason the Venetian merchants of 500 years ago were not satisfied with records of cash in and out alone is the same as for today’s properties. <strong>The amount sold and the amount remaining are different faces of the same property.</strong> And a property has a moment when it experiences these "two faces" daily: the deposit. A booking payment arrives in the account, but it is not yet the property’s money. The obligation to keep the promise of a stay increases at the same time. The spirit of double-entry shows best in <strong>the one line of the advance received</strong>.</p>

<h2>3 faces — revenue, gross profit, cash</h2>

<p>The monthly three numbers are three faces of the same property.</p>

<ol>
<li><strong>Revenue — the guest’s face</strong> — Count it split into rooms, F&B (breakfast, restaurant), and other (retail, experiences, day use). Split, it shows "what kind of property earns."</li>
<li><strong>Gross profit — the face of purchasing</strong> — What remains of revenue after cost of sales. Into costs go linen, laundry, food loss, and OTA commissions too (one of the hard-to-see costs handled by <a href="#ota-commission-visibility">Making OTA Commissions Visible</a>). If the gross-profit margin (gross profit ÷ revenue) falls, it is a sign that either costs or prices have moved.</li>
<li><strong>Cash — the wallet’s face</strong> — How much was left on hand this month. Revenue up and no cash left, and management is hard. Of the three faces, the most honest one.</li>
</ol>

<p>One deep reading of cash. Divide the cash on hand by "the amount that always goes out each month (fixed costs + repayments)," and out comes <strong>the number of months the property can live without revenue</strong> (a general way of thinking). There is an old saying — "profit is an opinion, cash is a fact" — and the existence of a word like "profitable bankruptcy" (going under while profitable) is precisely because these two are different faces.</p>

<h2>How to make the sheet — 12 rows and 1 line</h2>

<ol>
<li><strong>Lay the 3 numbers, 12 months, on one sheet</strong> — Paper or spreadsheet. The seasonal peaks and valleys show as rows.</li>
<li><strong>Compare with the same month last year</strong> — A property’s numbers swing hard with the seasons. The comparison partner is not last month but the same month last year.</li>
<li><strong>Write the reason for the change, in one line only</strong> — "F&B rose with the local beer"; "linen costs went up." Do not write long analyses; the reason stays one line. A long analysis, the three numbers will say in your place.</li>
</ol>

<h2>Just 2 lines of the system (as of writing)</h2>

<div class="stats"><span class="tag">FACT</span><strong>The special deduction for the "blue" (aiiro) tax filing is ¥650,000 with double-entry bookkeeping via e-Tax or electronic bookkeeping, ¥550,000 with paper filing</strong> — From the 2027 filing year, the deduction system is set to change: combined with proper electronic bookkeeping, up to ¥750,000; paper filing reduced to ¥100,000 (fiscal 2026 tax revision, per major accounting-service explanations). Also, receipts and invoices received by email or cloud must, since 2024, be kept as data (Electronic Bookkeeping Act).</div>

<p>That is all for tax. The heart of bookkeeping is not the return but the monthly three numbers.</p>

<h2>30 minutes in a quiet place</h2>

<p>Once a month, 30 minutes. Not the office — somewhere quiet: an understated café, the empty dining room in the morning. Over one cup of coffee, read the three numbers, and <strong>decide exactly one next move</strong>. If revenue’s face is weak, the sales channels; if gross profit’s face, the purchasing; if cash’s face, the costs. One move only. Past 30 minutes, the meeting does not last.</p>

<h2>The other side — 5 pitfalls of the 3 numbers</h2>

<ul>
<li><strong>Starting with perfect double-entry and breaking</strong> — First the three numbers. The full move to double-entry is not too late after the monthly sheet has run two or three times.</li>
<li><strong>Time eaten by the recording itself</strong> — Photograph receipts on the spot. For monthly entry, there is also the option of bookkeeping outsourcing.</li>
<li><strong>Only looking, and ending</strong> — A monthly review that decides no move is a photograph you merely gaze at.</li>
<li><strong>Feeling safe because cash is there</strong> — Advances received are promises still to keep. On hand is not necessarily free to spend.</li>
<li><strong>Only one person seeing the numbers</strong> — Noticing a change in condition takes both the person watching daily and the person watching monthly.</li>
</ul>

<p>The first step is laying out the past three months’ three numbers on one sheet at next month’s closing. The health check of a property starts from looking at the past. When twelve months are gathered, that one sheet becomes, for the first time, "the record of your family doctor."</p>

<h2>1. 需要关注的三个数字——销售额、毛利润和现金</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>销售——分为房间、餐饮和其他。与 12 个月日历比较：</strong>销售分为客房、F&B（早餐/餐厅）、其他（产品销售、体验、日间使用等）。我们关注整体销售额，不仅包括客房销售额，还包括餐饮和其他销售额。通过与 12 个月日历进行比较（参见文章），您可以了解与去年同月相比销售额是上升还是下降。</li>
<li><strong>毛利润——销售额减去商品成本。扣除床单、衣物和食物损失后的数字：</strong>毛利润是销售额减去商品成本。成本包括布草、洗衣、食物浪费、OTA费用等。毛利润是按“销售额-商品成本”计算的，同时也看毛利率（毛利润÷销售额）。如果毛利率下降，则是因为商品成本增加或单价下降。</li>
<li><strong>现金 — 您本月还剩下多少钱（现金，不是销售额）：</strong>销售额和现金是不同的。即使销售额增加，如果没有剩余现金，经营旅馆也很困难。按“销售额-成本-费用-还款”计算现金，看看你这个月还剩下多少钱。经营客栈最重要的指标就是你有没有现金。</li>
</ol>

<h2>2. 如何制作一张纸 — 将 3 个数字放在一张纸上，并与上一年的同月进行比较。</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>在 Excel 或纸上按月排列 3 个数字：</strong>在 Excel 或纸上按月排列三个数字（销售额、毛利润、现金）。通过排列 1 月至 12 月的三个月数字，您可以看到季节波动。查看季节性趋势将帮助您规划明年的预算。</li>
<li><strong>与去年同月比较（见12个月历）：</strong>3与上一年同月的数字进行比较。你可以看到销售额与去年同月相比是否有所增长，毛利润是否依然存在，是否还有现金剩余。与去年同月对比，可以看出客栈的成长。</li>
<li><strong>只写一行解释其增加/减少的原因：</strong>3写一行解释与去年同月相比数字增加或减少的原因。例如：“销售额增加，因为随着本地啤酒的推出，餐饮销售额增加。”“毛利润下降，因为亚麻布成本上升。”只写一行就决定了您的下一步行动。</li>
</ul>

<h2>3. 运营——每月在一个安静的咖啡馆般的地方举行一次30分钟的会议。</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>每月一次，召开一次 30 分钟的会议来查看三个数字：</strong>每月召开一次会议，花 30 分钟查看这三个数字。如果你做超过30分钟，它就会变得更长并且不会持续。 30 分钟内查看这三个数字，然后决定下一步行动。至于你的下一步行动，决定下个月之前要做的一件事。</li>
<li><strong>在凉爽的咖啡馆等安静的地方喝咖啡时：</strong>会议在咖啡馆等安静的地方举行，而不是在办公室，一边喝咖啡。在安静的地方喝咖啡的时候看着这三个数字，可以冷静地思考旅馆的管理。会议不仅是“看数字”，更是“思考旅馆的未来”。</li>
<li><strong>只需查看 3 个数字，您就可以决定下一步行动：</strong>记账的目的不是“纳税申报”，而是“旅馆健康检查”。只需查看 3 个数字，您就可以决定下一步行动。如果销售额下降，请增加销售渠道。如果毛利润下降，请审查成本。如果您没有剩余现金，请检查您的支出。看看这三个数字，就能看出客栈的管理面临的问题。</li>
</ul>
