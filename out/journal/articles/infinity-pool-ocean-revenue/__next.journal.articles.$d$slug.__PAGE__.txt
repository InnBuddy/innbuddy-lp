11:T1715,<p>As long as a view is treated as "a scenery that happens to be there," it earns nothing. A view becomes <strong>a "revenue account" only when it is designed</strong>. This article decodes the core of that design from the world’s most famous pool.</p>

<h2>On the 57th floor, the boundary disappears</h2>

<div class="stats"><span class="tag">CASE</span><strong>The Singapore integrated resort Marina Bay Sands (opened 2010, designed by the architect Moshe Safdie) holds, on the 57th floor of its SkyPark atop three hotel towers, about 200 meters above ground, a 150-meter infinity pool</strong> — Introduced by the resort as "the world’s largest rooftop infinity pool." The water is about 1.2 m deep, with a separate 0.5 m children’s pool. Use is for staying guests only (per the resort’s official guide).</div>

<p>Why it is interesting: that pool’s true identity is not equipment for swimming but <strong>"a device for erasing the boundary line."</strong> The mechanism is simple — the water level is kept just above the rim, and the overflow falls into a channel outside the rim and circulates. With the rim invisible, the water’s surface connects to sea and sky. What guests come to buy is not swimming but <strong>a single scenery whose border has vanished</strong>.</p>

<p>The essence: <strong>the technology that turns a view into revenue is erasing a boundary line.</strong> Erase one line between the sea and the property, and the scenery changes from "something looked at" into "something entered."</p>

<h2>3 accounts of a view</h2>

<ol>
<li><strong>Room price</strong> — Rooms that see the sea sell higher than rooms that do not. To know the difference in numbers, the foundation is <a href="#revenue-management-basics">Revenue Management Basics: How to Read ADR, Occupancy, and RevPAR</a>.</li>
<li><strong>Saved photographs</strong> — The frame with the border erased is a frame that gets saved (<a href="#instagram-saveable-account">Designing an Instagram Account for "Saves," Not Followers</a>).</li>
<li><strong>Time spent</strong> — The longer guests stay before the view, the more drinks and food they add. The view is a department that keeps selling after the room is sold.</li>
</ol>

<h2>Translation — erase the border even without a pool</h2>

<p>Here is the part that matters to small properties. The design of erasing the boundary is not the privilege of giant pools.</p>

<ul>
<li><strong>Align the water’s surface with the horizon</strong> — Let the open-air bath’s surface appear to merge with the sea’s horizon. Place no line between the bath and the sea.</li>
<li><strong>Keep window mullions and rails below the sightline</strong> — Not one horizontal bar above the eye of a seated guest. What makes the border is, usually, the handrail.</li>
<li><strong>Place nothing before the view</strong> — Keep a third as margin. And set out one chair only — a scenery that invites sitting needs just one place to sit.</li>
<li><strong>A water basin in the garden</strong> — At properties that cannot see the sea, one low basin lets the water’s surface mirror the sky and creates "a second horizon."</li>
</ul>

<p>In photographs, capture that boundary line in one frame (<a href="#room-photography-guide">How to Photograph Guest Rooms: The Two Points of Composition and Light</a>). And do not forget the one spoken line — "from this window, the sea is at its stillest tomorrow at 6 a.m." A line that tells the best hour of the view is a line that puts the scenery on the calendar.</p>

<h2>The other side — 5 lines for handling a view</h2>

<ul>
<li><strong>Safety</strong> — Water’s edges and children, night and drinking guests: exclude the combinations from the design, first. Even the world’s largest pool separates the children’s at 0.5 m. What to imitate is not the look but the line-drawing.</li>
<li><strong>Cleaning</strong> — Outdoor water gathers sea breeze, algae, and fallen leaves. The circulation and filtration design is half the beauty.</li>
<li><strong>Running costs</strong> — Circulation-pump electricity, water, chemicals, specialist inspections. Do not forget the automatic debit from the view’s account that running costs represent.</li>
<li><strong>Regulations and confirmation</strong> — Installing a pool involves building confirmation and hygiene standards. Implementation presupposes confirmation with the authorities.</li>
<li><strong>The gap between photo and reality</strong> — Raise expectations with retouching, and reviews shave them back. Telling the "best hour" rather than retouching makes a view’s reputation last longer.</li>
</ul>

<p>The first step is sitting tomorrow morning in the chair with the best sea view in the house. Measure the eye’s height, and write out one thing above it that makes the border (a handrail, a transom, the curtain rail). Erasing one boundary line — that is the procedure for opening the view’s account.</p>

<h2>避免失败的3个要点</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>从小处开始——先做好一件事：</strong>一开始就想全部改变，往往难以持续。先从一件事开始。可以从一间客房、一个货架或一句话开始；即使效果不理想，影响也小，更容易调整。</li>
<li><strong>记录——用一张表看清楚：</strong>把行动记录在一张表里，效果就能被看见。你会知道哪些做法有效、哪些需要调整，记录也会成为下一轮改进的依据。</li>
<li><strong>持续——每周一次，每次15分钟：</strong>新行动需要持续。每周留出15分钟，逐渐形成习惯，最后才会成为团队文化。</li>
</ol>
