1b:T1e59,<p>Pet-friendly is <strong>not "allowed / not allowed" but "the design of 3 elements."</strong> The single line "pets allowed" is a seed of trouble. Which room, at what price, under what rules you welcome them — design these three, and pet-friendly stays stop being "a source of trouble" and become "a mechanism that creates regulars." This article is the foundation of the three elements, starting from one room.</p>

<div class="stats"><span class="tag">FACT</span><strong>In 2023, the number of dogs and cats kept nationwide was estimated at about 15.91 million combined (dogs about 6.84 million, cats about 9.07 million) (Japan Pet Food Association, "National Survey of Dog and Cat Ownership"). This exceeds the same year’s population of about 14.17 million children under 15 (Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications population estimate)</strong> — Traveling with pets is not a hobby of a few guests but demand standing in the mainstream of family makeup.</div>

<h2>Properties once stabled horses</h2>

<p>Put the viewpoint in history. At the post stations on the highways, travelers walked with their horses. In post towns, stones for tethering horses and the remains of stables can still be found. A property, in other words, over most of history, was <strong>a place that welcomed the companion along with the person</strong>. The "rooms-only property" lodging humans alone is, historically, the newcomer. This fact flips the order of thinking. Accepting pets is not "whether to add a special service" but <strong>whether to redesign the property’s oldest work for the modern family</strong>.</p>

<h2>The 3 elements — staying, price, rules</h2>

<ol>
<li><strong>Staying — one room only, first-floor corner, with a terrace</strong> — There is no need to make every room pet-friendly. One room on the ground floor, with direct outdoor access from a terrace or a private entrance. The condition is that it can be separated, circulation included, from guests uncomfortable with animals.</li>
<li><strong>Price — cleaning included, separate, and clear</strong> — For example ¥3,000 per pet per night (cleaning included; a reference value). Free makes it look like "an effortless service"; too high, and the "welcome" disappears. Explaining the extra charge in one line — "payment for the extra cleaning labor" — connects to the next element.</li>
<li><strong>Rules — on one sheet: what is allowed and what is not</strong> — "In the room, yes. In the building, in a carrier or in your arms. Dining room and hot springs, no. Walks, on the designated course." Not a long text — one sheet. List not only prohibitions but <strong>first, what is possible</strong>. The clearer the rules, the more at ease those asked to keep them.</li>
</ol>

<h2>Telling — a photograph, a map, and one more</h2>

<ul>
<li><strong>One photograph is enough</strong> — A room with a pet bed placed in it. It is "proof that my child is welcome," a role different from an amenity list.</li>
<li><strong>A map of walking courses</strong> — The center of a trip with a pet is, in fact, outside the property. The design of 15-, 30-, 60-minute walking courses on a map can be used as is from <a href="#idea-walk-local-15-30-60">Convey Region with Walk - Ideas for 15, 30, 60 Minute Walks Starting from Inn</a>.</li>
<li><strong>An honest note about smells and fur</strong> — Write that most rooms remain pet-free and that cleaning is separated. The explanation to guests without pets is part of the "pet-friendly" design too.</li>
</ul>

<div class="stats"><span class="tag">DID YOU KNOW?</span><strong>Guide dogs, service dogs, and hearing dogs are not "pets." Their acceptance at lodging facilities is stipulated by the Act on Assistance Dogs for Physically Disabled Persons</strong> — A framework separate from the pets-allowed debate. The basics of acceptance are handled by <a href="#barrier-free-elderly-family">Barrier-Free Basics for the Elderly, the Disabled, and Families</a>.</div>

<h2>Cleaning and insurance — the backstage of welcoming</h2>

<ul>
<li><strong>Cleaning on a separate checklist</strong> — Pet-friendly rooms run on a dedicated list with checks added for fur, smell, and damage. Estimate cleaning time about 15 minutes longer than usual. The craft of visible cleaning is handled by <a href="#cleaning-checklist-experience">Turning Housekeeping From "Cost" Into "Experience": A Checklist</a>.</li>
<li><strong>Checking insurance</strong> — Broken fixtures, trouble between pets, guest injuries. What is covered — check once with <a href="#hotel-insurance-risk">Hotel Insurance: Risks to Know Before They Happen</a>, before starting.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Properties it fits, properties it does not</h2>

<p>This mechanism shows its power at properties with a ground-floor room with a terrace, grounds with room to spare, and roads suited to walks. Conversely, at properties where all rooms are carpeted and the common spaces are close, even starting from one room may bring worry first. <strong>Writing honestly "we cannot welcome pets" is also an omotenashi that spares others trouble.</strong> There is no need to blame the decision not to welcome; all that is needed is to design carefully the side that does the welcoming.</p>

<h2>The other side — when welcoming exhausts the property</h2>

<ul>
<li><strong>Coexisting with uncomfortable guests</strong> — Separate the circulation, and smells and barking still do not separate easily. The discomfort may be allergy, or it may be fear. Keep "separation" and "ventilation" always paired.</li>
<li><strong>Late-night barking</strong> — To the guest next door, it is a matter of sleep, not of pets. A property’s posture of protecting sound sleep weighs the same as consideration for those with pets.</li>
<li><strong>The boundary of accident and liability</strong> — Escapes, bites, broken fixtures. "We took the pet in because asked" and "it happened on the premises" put liability in different places. Drawing the line — we do not take custody — is also an option.</li>
<li><strong>The trap of turning regulars</strong> — "Feel at home, as if it were your own room" can also mean familiarity loosening the rules of the place. Precisely because they are regulars, keep the rules constant.</li>
</ul>

<p>The first step is choosing one room. Ground floor, direct access outside — one room only. Place one pet bed in it, write the rules on one sheet, add a map of the walking course. As properties stabled horses through history, welcome guests who come as a family, starting from one room — the design of pet-friendly stays is the newest edition of the property’s oldest work.</p>

1c:T1a5a,<p>允许携带宠物<strong>“三要素设计”而不是“可能/不可能”</strong>。对于想要带着宠物住在靠近日本乡村、大海或山脉的旅馆的人来说，肯定有这种需求。简单地写上“允许携带宠物”可能会引起麻烦。哪些房间允许携带宠物，价格是多少，以及如何传达规则。通过这三个要素的设计，带宠物就不会成为麻烦的根源，而是一个会带来常客的系统。本文从一间房间开始，概述了宠物友好型设计的三个要素。</p>

<h2>1. 3要素——住宿、价格、规则</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>住宿 — 哪些房间允许携带宠物：</strong>并非所有房间都需要允许携带宠物。一楼只有一间带露台和私人入口的客房允许携带宠物入住。将宠物友好的客房与不喜欢宠物的客人分开非常重要。理想情况下，一楼的角落房间可以从露台直接通往外面。宠物友好型客房设有“特殊清洁清单”，可彻底检查毛皮和气味。</li>
<li><strong>价格——单独的宠物费（包括清洁费）：</strong>宠物费将单独说明为“含清洁费”。示例：每只动物每晚 3,000 日元（含清洁费）。通过明确宠物费用，携带宠物的客人知道需要支付额外费用，而没有宠物的客人知道他们允许携带宠物，但已打扫干净。宠物费被解释为“清洁工作”并作为参考值呈现。</li>
<li><strong>规则 — 一张纸上可否使用设施、餐厅和温泉的信息：</strong>携带宠物的规则应该总结在一张纸上，而不是长句子。内容要简洁，例如“可以留在客房内，可以将孩子抱在笼子里或抱在酒店内，不能去餐厅或温泉，可以在指定路线上散步。”规则没有规定禁止做什么，但可以做什么和不能做什么。明确的规则可以减少麻烦。</li>
</ol>

<h2>2. 沟通——一张照片、一条规则和一个步行路线</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>照片 — 包括 1 张宠物床/宠物设施：</strong>在客房照片中添加宠物床或宠物用品。当携带宠物的客人看到有宠物床的照片时，就会觉得这家酒店欢迎宠物入住。允许携带宠物的客房的照片应与普通客房照片分开准备，以便携带宠物的客人更容易理解。</li>
<li><strong>将规则总结在一张纸上（不要太长）：</strong>携带宠物的规则应该总结在一张纸上，而不是长句子。我们将在您办理登机手续时将其交给您并口头向您解释。规则不是用来阅读的，而是用来传达的。通过将规则总结在一张纸上，我们将鼓励客人遵守规则。</li>
<li><strong>提供附近步行路线的地图（山脉、海洋、自然）：</strong>携带宠物的客人需要步行道。通过提供附近步行路线（山、海、自然）的地图，客人将很高兴能够与他们的宠物一起散步。对于携带宠物的客人来说，步行路线地图是一种“享受”。如果步行道包括宠物友好的公园和狗跑道，那就更受欢迎了。</li>
</ul>

<h2>3. 清洁、保险、定期保养</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>清洁 — 宠物友好房间的单独清洁清单（毛发/气味）：</strong>宠物友好型客房的清洁清单与普通客房不同。彻底检查是否有毛发、气味和划痕。清洁清单将与我们的清洁人员共享，他们将估计宠物友好型客房的清洁时间比平时多 15 分钟。彻底的清洁让不带宠物的客人可以安心入住。</li>
<li><strong>保险确认（参见旅馆保险文章）：</strong>对于允许携带宠物的住宿，必须确认保险。检查您的保险是否承保宠物造成的设备损坏、宠物之间的问题、宠物造成的客人受伤等。在出现任何问题之前检查您的保险非常重要。</li>
<li><strong>正规化——重复宠物友好住宿：</strong>宠物友好的住宿对于携带宠物的客人来说很有价值。允许携带宠物的住宿很少，因此，如果您在那里住过一次并且喜欢它，那么您很有可能会再次回来。通过培养携带宠物的常客，您可以产生稳定的收入。携带宠物的常客可以像使用自己的房间一样使用宠物友好型客房。</li>
</ul>
