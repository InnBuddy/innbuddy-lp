9:T1d49,<p>日本でいちばん早く起きる街は、港です。夜明け前、魚を積んだ船が戻り、氷が振りかけられ、せりの声が上がります。この朝の風景は、宿が朝食前に案内できる、いちばん早く、いちばん生きた観光資源です。この記事は、港町の朝を体験にするための、時間割と作法と安全の線を扱います。</p>

<h2>1. 数字——港の9割は、誰かの仕事場です</h2>
<p>漁港法に基づく指定漁港は、全国で2,785港（2021年4月時点、国土交通省北海道開発局の集計）。そのうち第1種漁港——利用が地元の漁業を主とする港——が2,047港を占めます。<strong>港の9割以上は、地元の漁師さんの仕事場</strong>です。観光地向けに整備された港はごく一部で、ゲストが訪れる港のほとんどは、毎朝誰かの暮らしがかかった場所です。この事実は、港の案内の作法そのものを決めているのです。港は見学する場所であり、立ち入る場所ではありません。</p>
<div class="stats"><span class="tag">FACT</span><strong>全国2,785港の内訳</strong> — 第1種（地元利用）2,047港／第2種（やや広域）525港／第3種（全国的利用）101港／特定第3種13港／第4種（離島・辺地）99港（2021年4月1日現在）。特定第3種はわずか13港——広域流通を担う大港と、地域を支える小港。港町の旅の中心になるのは、たいてい後者である。</div>

<h2>2. 時間割——せりは、なぜ朝5時半なのでしょうか</h2>
<p>東京・豊洲市場では、一般来場者は朝5時から入場でき、マグロのせりはおおむね5時30分から6時30分の1時間で終わります。開始を知らせる鐘が鳴り、せり人と仲買人の手信号だけで、数十キロから数百キロのマグロが次々に決まっていくのです。築地時代は人数限定だった見学も、豊洲では2階の見学者通路から誰でも見られます（豊洲市場の案内による）。</p>
<p>せりが朝いちばんなのは、惰習ではありません。<strong>その日の魚を、その日のうちに店頭に並べるには、朝の数時間で価格を決め、さばき、流通に乗せるしかない</strong>からです。港の朝の時間割とは、鮮度という物理でできています。港町の宿の体験設計も、この時間割に合わせるしかありません——「港を見に行くなら6時です」と案内できる宿が、港町の宿です。水揚げとせりを見て戻ったゲストに、7時半の朝食で魚を出しましょう。この90分の流れが、港町の宿の黄金の朝です。</p>

<h2>3. 漁師の話——話を聞くことは、記録を残すこと</h2>
<p>港町の宿のもうひとつの財産は、漁師さんの話です。魚種の季節、海の荒れ方、漁具の変化、若い頃の話——これらは観光の素材ではなく、<strong>地域の漁業の口述記録</strong>です。話を冊子にする、ボードに書く、録音を残す（了承を得て）——聞き書きの形にすることで、話は「おもしろい話」から「この港の記録」になります。泊まりに来た子どもが漁師さんの話を聞く15分は、教科書に載らない水産業の授業です。</p>
<p>道具も同じです。浮き、網、釣針、羅針盤、船具——廊下に飾るときは名札をつけましょう（<a href="#idea-craftsman-handwork">職人の手仕事</a>の記事で扱った原則の、海版です）。網の補修の実演が見られれば、網は「編み続けられている道具」だと分かります。</p>

<h2>4. 制度——「海の駅」という、港の開け方</h2>
<div class="stats"><span class="tag">CASE</span><strong>「海の駅」は、港を来訪者に開く制度である</strong> — 国土交通省が推進する「海の駅」は、船舶係留施設・ガイド・トイレの3つを備えたマリンレジャーの拠点で、2026年3月末時点で全国179か所が登録されている。道の駅が道路利用者のために生まれたように、海の駅は海と陸の接点として港を開いた。漁港そのものではないが、海の駅に登録された港では、来訪者を受け入れる準備が整っている——港町の宿が提携先を探すとき、まず当たるべき制度である。</div>

<h2>5. 安全と衛生——港は、工場です</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>立入禁止は、命の線</strong> — 漁港ではクレーン・フォークリフト・トラックが動き、地面は濡れて滑り、氷と海水で冷えます。「荷捌きエリアに入らない」「呼ばれない場所に行かない」を、案内の最初に書きましょう。</li>
<li><strong>せり・市場の見学マナー</strong> — せりの間は声を出さない、写真はフラッシュなし（競りの士気を削がない）、通路を塞がないようにします。これも見学の作法です。</li>
<li><strong>魚の衛生</strong> — さばき方体験や魚の試食では、温度管理と鮮度が主題です。生食の魚は氷で冷やし続け、時間が経ったら加熱に回します。体験の設計は、最初に所轄の保健所に相談するのが正しい順番です。</li>
<li><strong>船に乗る体験</strong> — 釣りや潮干狩りの船を出すなら、遊漁船業の届出を受けた事業者・船に頼むのが基本です。ライフジャケットは大人にも。</li>
<li><strong>天候で全部変わる</strong> — 水揚げは海の機嫌次第。「今日は何も上がりませんでした」もあります。港の体験案内は、成果を約束しない書き方にしておきましょう。</li>
</ul>
<div class="note"><span class="tag">InnBuddy VIEW</span>港町の宿の案内カードには、時間割を書きましょう。何時に船が戻り、何時にせりが始まり、何時に片付くのでしょうか。<strong>港の朝は毎日開かれる、予約不要の一大行事</strong>——時間を知っているだけで、誰でも見に行けます。港町の宿の実力は、ベッドの数ではなく、この時間割を案内できるかどうかで決まります。</div>

11:T1eb8,<p>The street that wakes earliest in Japan is the port. Before dawn, the boats come home laden with fish, ice rains down, and the calls of the auction rise. This morning scenery is the earliest and the most alive of the tourist resources an inn can guide before breakfast. This article treats the timetable, the manners, and the lines of safety for turning a port town’s morning into an experience.</p>

<h2>1. The figures — 90% of ports are somebody’s workplace</h2>
<p>The designated fishing ports under the Fishing Port Act number 2,785 nationwide (as of April 2021, per the tally of the Hokkaido Regional Development Bureau, Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism). Of these, Category 1 fishing ports — ports whose use is mainly by the local fisheries — account for 2,047. <strong>More than 90% of ports are the workplaces of the local fishermen.</strong> Ports developed for tourism are a mere handful, and nearly every port a guest visits is a place where somebody’s living is at stake each morning. This fact decides the manners of port guidance themselves. The port is a place to observe, not a place to enter.</p>
<div class="stats"><span class="tag">FACT</span><strong>The breakdown of the 2,785 ports nationwide</strong> — Category 1 (local use) 2,047 ports / Category 2 (somewhat wider area) 525 / Category 3 (nationwide use) 101 / Specified Category 3, 13 / Category 4 (remote islands, outlying areas) 99 (as of April 1, 2021). Specified Category 3 ports are only 13 — the great ports that carry wide-area distribution, and the small ports that support the region. The center of a port-town trip is, most often, the latter.</div>

<h2>2. The timetable — why the auction is at 5:30 in the morning</h2>
<p>At Tokyo’s Toyosu Market, general visitors may enter from 5 a.m., and the tuna auction is, as a rule, finished within the one hour from 5:30 to 6:30. The bell announcing the start rings out, and by the hand signals of the auctioneer and the brokers alone, tuna from tens to hundreds of kilograms are decided one after another. The viewing that was limited by headcount in the Tsukiji days can, at Toyosu, be done by anyone from the second-floor visitors’ corridor (per the Toyosu Market guide).</p>
<p>That the auction comes at the first hour of the morning is no mere habit of long standing. <strong>To put that day’s fish on that day’s counters, there is no way but to fix the prices, cut the fish, and load them onto distribution within the few morning hours.</strong> The port’s morning timetable is built of the physics called freshness. The experience design of a port-town inn, too, can only be matched to this timetable — an inn that can guide "if you are going to see the port, six o’clock is the hour" is a port-town inn. Serve fish at the 7:30 breakfast to the guests back from watching the landing and the auction. This flow of ninety minutes is the port-town inn’s golden morning.</p>

<h2>3. The fishermen’s talk — to listen is to leave a record</h2>
<p>The other asset of a port-town inn is the fishermen’s talk. The seasons of each fish, the ways the sea turns rough, the changes of fishing gear, the stories of their youth — these are not sightseeing material but <strong>the oral record of the region’s fisheries.</strong> Make a booklet, write them on a board, keep the recordings (with consent) — given the form of the recorded interview, a story changes from "an interesting tale" into "a record of this port." The fifteen minutes a child staying at the inn spends listening to a fisherman is a class in the fishing industry that appears in no textbook.</p>
<p>The tools are the same. Floats, nets, hooks, the compass, the ship’s fittings — when they are hung in the corridor, give each its name tag (the sea version of the principle treated in <a href="#idea-craftsman-handwork">Touch Craftsman's Handwork - Ideas for Showing Handwork of Carpenter, Plasterer, Joiner, Tatami</a>). If a mending of the nets can be watched, the net becomes visible as "a tool still being woven."</p>

<h2>4. The institution — "sea stations," a way of opening the port</h2>
<div class="stats"><span class="tag">CASE</span><strong>The "sea station" is a system that opens ports to visitors</strong> — The "sea stations" promoted by the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism are bases for marine leisure equipped with three things: vessel mooring facilities, guides, and toilets. As of the end of March 2026, 179 places nationwide were registered. Just as the roadside stations were born for road users, the sea stations opened the ports as the points where sea meets land. They are not fishing ports themselves, but at ports registered as sea stations, the preparation for receiving visitors is in place — the first system to turn to when a port-town inn looks for partners.</div>

<h2>5. Safety and hygiene — the port is a factory</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>No-entry zones are lines that guard life</strong> — In a fishing port, cranes, forklifts, and trucks move; the ground is wet and slippery; ice and seawater chill. Write "Do not enter the cargo-handling area" and "Do not go where you are not called" at the head of the guidance.</li>
<li><strong>Manners for watching the auction and the market</strong> — Raise no voice during the auction; photographs without flash (do not dent the bidding’s spirit); block no corridor. These too are the manners of watching.</li>
<li><strong>Hygiene of the fish</strong> — In filleting experiences and fish tastings, temperature control and freshness are the theme. Keep raw-eaten fish chilled on ice the whole time; when time passes, turn it to cooked use. The right order in designing the experience is to consult the health office with jurisdiction first.</li>
<li><strong>Experiences on boats</strong> — For putting out boats for fishing or shellfish gathering, the basics are to rely on an operator or vessel covered by the fishing-boat business notification. Life jackets for adults too.</li>
<li><strong>The weather changes everything</strong> — The catch depends on the sea’s mood. "Nothing came in today" happens too. Write the port experience guide in words that promise no result.</li>
</ul>
<div class="note"><span class="tag">InnBuddy VIEW</span>What the guide card of a port-town inn should carry is the timetable. What time the boats return, what time the auction begins, what time it is cleared away. <strong>The port’s morning is a great event opened every day, no reservation needed</strong> — knowing the time, anyone can go and see. The strength of a port-town inn is decided not by the number of beds but by whether it can guide this timetable.</div>

For activities at the water’s edge, include safety measures and the preparation of clothing and tools in the design.</div>
